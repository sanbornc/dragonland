# Session 06 — The Promise of Mishakal

> **Session in progress (live log).** Opening state: the party is in
> **[Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md)**, on the road south from
> **[Solace](../wiki/locations/solace.md)** toward **[Haven](../wiki/locations/haven.md)**,
> in the immediate aftermath of the draconian ambush
> ([Session 05](05-2026-07-07-the-missing-stars.md)). Four
> **[draconians](../wiki/npcs/draconians.md)** dead; the real
> **[Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md)** still with
> **[Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md)**;
> **[Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)** revived a third time, bound and
> furious; **[Vaelor](../wiki/characters/vaelor-grinward.md)'s axe** trapped in a
> petrified draconian; the fight went loud (Fireball + an alerted search party) in a wood
> the party knows to be haunted by undead. The party is **marked for death by
> [Lord Seeker Elistan](../wiki/npcs/elistan.md)** and publicly branded fugitives, their
> Solace HQ burned.

## Recap

<!-- live log — appended as the session runs -->

- **Deeper into [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md), the wood turns wrong.**
  Pressing on through the forest, the party notices three things at once:
  - **Their shadows are behaving strangely** — **delayed**, lagging behind the bodies that
    cast them, and **not always falling in the right direction.**
  - **Their trail disappears behind them** — the wood is closing over their tracks as they
    pass. *(Cuts both ways: the draconians' charcoal wagon-trail map may be useless here —
    but so is any hope of retracing their own path out, or of anyone finding them.)*
  - **Fighting can be heard in the distance** — an unseen battle somewhere in the wood.
    *(Unresolved: who is fighting whom — the alerted goblin search party, more
    [draconians](../wiki/npcs/draconians.md), the wood's undead, or something else?)*
- **The wood's reputation: those who enter Darken Wood don't come back.** Party knowledge /
  regional rumor — beyond "haunted by undead," the wood is held to simply **keep** whoever
  walks into it. The vanishing trail behind them gives that rumor a mechanism.
- **The party presses *toward* the fighting** — and finds
  **shadow figures vs. [Knights of Solamnia](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md).**
  Living Solamnic knights, in the flesh, in the middle of Darken Wood — the order the
  [Seekers](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) work to keep away from the
  [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md), and whose relics the party has been collecting since
  the buried archive. Their enemy: **shadow figures** — plausibly the same phenomenon as the
  party's **delayed, wrong-facing shadows** in this wood.
- **The party watches it like spectators at a sporting event** — approaching not as
  combatants but as **fans**, and **split on who they were cheering for.** (No side taken
  yet; the fight is being *watched*, not joined.)
- **A knight noticed them — and mistook them for new recruits**, ordering them to
  **"hold the line."** The spectator bit collapses into conscription: the party is being
  **read as Solamnic** and given a battle order. *(A free in with the
  [Knights of Solamnia](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) — the one faction the
  [Seekers](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) work to keep away from the
  [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md) — if the party plays along.)*
- **The scale of it: ~50 knights holding against seemingly endless waves of shadows.**
  Not a skirmish — a **battle line** in the middle of Darken Wood, the knights on the
  defensive against an enemy that keeps coming. An attrition fight the knights cannot win by
  killing, only outlast. *(Open: are these living knights making a stand, or the wood's own
  "those who enter don't return" — a battle that has been going on far longer than it looks?)*
- **The knight faded away mid-conversation — and the battle *reset*.** The knight who
  conscripted them **vanished while they were still speaking to him**, and the whole
  engagement **started over from the beginning.** The battle is a **loop**, not a battle:
  the knights are not living defenders holding a line but a **recurring apparition** of one,
  replaying. It answers the wood's reputation — *those who enter don't return* — and the
  earlier omens (**delayed, wrong-facing shadows**; the **trail closing behind them**) read
  as the same phenomenon: **Darken Wood does not let things end.** *(Open: is the loop
  bound to a moment, a place, or an unfinished task — and can it be broken, or joined?)*
- **The loop comes around — and the knight gives a name: [Emrich](../wiki/npcs/emrich.md).**
  He reapproaches with **no memory of the previous cycle**, and again asks the party to
  **join ranks.** The repetition is exact: same knight, same ask, clean slate each time.
  *(First name recovered from the loop — a thread to pull. Same conscription offer, now
  from a named [Solamnic](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) knight.)*
- **Asked his orders and the year, [Emrich](../wiki/npcs/emrich.md) answers: it is the
  year of the Cataclysm.** He is holding the line **against the enemy**, under orders, in a
  year that ended **centuries ago.** He does not know the Cataclysm has happened — he is
  fighting inside the moment the world broke, over and over. The loop is **pinned to the
  Cataclysm itself.**
  - This dates the wood's haunting to the same era as the party's other Solamnic finds: the
    **pre-Cataclysm military archive** the [Black Banners](../wiki/factions/black-banners.md)
    are digging out ([The Buried Archive](../wiki/quests/the-buried-archive.md)) and
    **[Sir Garreck](../wiki/npcs/sir-garreck.md)**, the centuries-dead Solamnic guardian
    still at his post in the tower. **Two Solamnic knights, in two places, both still on
    duty from before the Cataclysm.**
  - It also bears on the open question in
    [Knights of Solamnia](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) — *did the order fall
    with the old kingdoms before the Cataclysm?* Emrich is a knight who fell **at** it.
- **Told the truth, [Emrich](../wiki/npcs/emrich.md) was *not surprised*.** On some level he
  already knew. What he volunteered instead was a confession:
  - **He sounded the horn for retreat when the mountain fell.** At the moment of the
    Cataclysm — *the mountain falling* — Emrich, who held the horn, **called the retreat**
    rather than the stand.
  - **He wonders whether this is his punishment** — condemned to hold the same line
    forever, never able to retreat and never able to win, because he once ordered men to
    break and run.
  - This gives the loop a **cause**, and it is not the wood: it is **guilt.** The
    unfinished thing pinning ~50 knights to the moment of the Cataclysm is one man's belief
    that he failed his oath. *(Which points at **absolution** rather than combat as the way
    out — and the party is escorting a working priest of a pre-Cataclysm god, plus a
    [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md) that performs genuine miracles.)*
  - **New Cataclysm detail: "the mountain fell."** The first description in the repo of what
    the Cataclysm looked like on the ground, from someone who was there. —
    [The Dragon Prophecy](../wiki/lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md) · [Knights of Solamnia](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md)
  - *(Open: is there a **horn** — still on him, still somewhere in the wood? And is the
    "punishment" self-imposed, or laid on him by something or someone?)*
- **🕊️ [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) absolved him — and the
  loop broke.** He convinced [Emrich](../wiki/npcs/emrich.md) that **sounding the retreat
  was not a failure of the oath**: with the mountain falling and the world ending, the order
  that got men out alive was the right one. Emrich accepted it.
  - **The knights faded into the forest and the shadows disappeared.** The battle that had
    been resetting for **three centuries** ended — not by killing the shadows, but by
    releasing the man whose guilt was generating them. ~50 Solamnic knights laid down a
    line they had held since the Cataclysm.
  - **The wood's curse is answered.** "Those who enter Darken Wood don't return," the
    delayed and wrong-facing shadows, the trail closing behind them — all of it traced to a
    loop pinned to the moment of the Cataclysm by one knight's belief that he had failed.
    *(Open: is the whole wood clear now, or only this battle?)*
  - **A working priest resolved what steel could not.** The party's answer to an endless
    undead attrition fight was **absolution, not combat** — divine power used as a *priest*
    uses it, in a world whose [Seekers](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) insist that power
    is dead and heretical. Boilerdang gave last rites, three hundred years late, to a knight
    of a god the world stopped believing in. —
    [Divine power in the world](../wiki/lore/divine-power.md) ·
    [The Silent Gods & the Seekers](../wiki/quests/the-silent-gods.md)

  **surrounded the party's [wagons](../wiki/items/party-wagon.md)** — the first living
  inhabitants of [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md) the party has met.
  Surrounded, not attacked. *(Unresolved: are they the wood's keepers, drawn by the loop
  breaking, or another party in the staff-hunt? Note the party is holding
  [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md), the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md), and a
  bound [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) in those wagons.)*
- **The centaur leader: *"that did not turn out as I expected."*** He seemed **impressed**,
  and asked the party to **follow him to meet the Forest Master.**
  - The phrasing means the centaurs were **watching** — they knew about
    [Emrich](../wiki/npcs/emrich.md)'s loop and **expected the party to fail** (or to be
    swallowed by it, like everyone else who enters). Breaking it was the unexpected outcome.
    Whether the encounter was a **test** the party unknowingly passed is open.
  - **New authority named: the "Forest Master"** — someone the centaurs answer to and
    consider worth bringing outsiders before. First indication that
    [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md) has a **living power** ruling it, not
    just a haunting. *(New NPC — create a wiki entry at distillation.)*
  - The party is being **escorted, not arrested** — but they are still surrounded, deep in a
    wood that historically does not release people, with
    [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md), the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md), and a
    bound [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) in tow.
- **The party accepted the escort** and followed the centaurs deeper into
  [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md) to meet the **Forest Master** — wagons,
  [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md), the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md), and the
  bound [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) and all.
  *(Scene in progress.)*
- **🦄 The Forest Master is a unicorn.** The party entered a clearing to find the wood's
  ruling power is a **unicorn** — a being of exactly the old, good, pre-Cataclysm order the

  - A bear the party spared out of mercy was **wearing an amulet** — which means it was
    **put on her by someone**, and she has been carrying it, apparently waiting to hand it

- **⭐ The Forest Master on the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md).** Asked what it is, the
  unicorn answered that it is **not merely a weapon or a vessel of healing** — it is
  **a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people; the people have forgotten the
  gods.**
  - This is a **direct answer to the campaign's central question.** The
    [Seekers](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) built their power on the claim that the gods
    died or abandoned the world at the Cataclysm. The Forest Master inverts it: **the
    abandonment ran the other way.** The silence was never the gods'.
  - It explains why the Staff is hunted so hard. Hederick's read (Session 05) was that its
    miracles threaten the Seekers' *legitimacy* — the unicorn goes further: the Staff is
    **evidence**, deliberately left, that the Seekers' founding doctrine is a lie.
  - It also explains **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)** —
    a cleric whose prayers are answered in a world that says they can't be — and dovetails
    with the **[missing constellations](../wiki/lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md)** (Session 05):
    the gods are not absent, they are **moving.**
