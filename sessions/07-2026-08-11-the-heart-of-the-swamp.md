# Session 07 — The Heart of the Swamp

## Recap

**Where we left off (Session 06 cliffhanger):** the party is **seven islands deep into
[the Drowned Swamp](../wiki/locations/the-drowned-swamp.md)**, following the submerged stone
roads toward **[Xak Tsaroth](../wiki/locations/xak-tsaroth.md)** on the
[Forest Master](../wiki/npcs/the-forest-master.md)'s maps, with
[Riverwind](../wiki/npcs/riverwind.md) guiding. The seventh "island" is larger and the fog is
thicker. They looted a **dead [Solamnic knight](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md)**
(sword + crested shield) and [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) gave
him last rites. Then **ripples moved in the water, the ground contracted, and a tendril rose.**

**⚔️ Session 07 opens on initiative.**

- **⚠️ It is not one tendril.** Looking down, the party sees **shapes beneath the water** they
  first take for **roots** — until the roots **move.** They are **tentacles**, plural, all
  around the island. The party is standing on **one creature**, and it has surrounded them.

- **⚔️ Initiative.** **[Pip](../wiki/characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md)** goes first — two arrows into a single
  tentacle for **30 damage** total.
- **🧿 The creature is resistant to poison and acid** — revealed when Pip marked it with
  **Hunter's Mark.**
- **💔 [Renob](../wiki/characters/renob.md) shoved
  [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) into the water** — into the tentacles,
  during the fight. *(Motive not stated at the table.)*
- **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) casts Spirit Guardians** and
  moves into range — the creature takes **19 damage** and **slips below the water.** Boilerdang
  follows with **Aid**.
- **⚠️ Four tentacles erupt from the water** at once — and
  **[Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) disappears beneath the surface.**
- **[Vaelor](../wiki/characters/vaelor-grinward.md) is hit** by a tentacle, and answers on his
  turn with **43 damage** to the one that struck him.
- **The tentacle Vaelor hit is bloodied; the others are not** — confirming **damage is tracked
  per tentacle, not pooled** into one body.
- **Pip's Hunter's Mark dropped off the tentacle that submerged.** *(The party read this as the
  tentacle having died — inference, not confirmed by the DM.)*
- **⚠️ A second creature begins to emerge from the water**, and a **cloud of noxious gas** spills
  across the island — **poisoned condition in a 20-ft radius.**
- **Only [Riverwind](../wiki/npcs/riverwind.md) was caught in the gas.** Boilerdang **pulled him
  clear and cast Lesser Restoration** on him. Goldmoon, the Staff, and the rest of the
  travellers were outside the radius.
- **✅ Several tentacles killed.** They *can* be cut down.
- **⚠️ A second burst of noxious gas** — this one **poisons Boilerdang.** The **original cloud**
  catches **[Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md)**, poisoning her as well.
- **⚠️ Two mud elementals climb out of the water onto the island** — a third kind of enemy in the
  same ambush.
- **A surge of water washes over the island.** **The whole party failed their CON saves** and
  took **8 damage** each — **and were all knocked prone.**
- **Spirit Guardians kills another tentacle** — then **Boilerdang loses concentration** and the
  aura drops.
- **⚠️ The main tentacled creature erupts up from beneath them** — the body itself, out of the
  water at last.
- **🗝️ The creature is named: the "Heart of the Swamp."** Not a species — a **title**, the same
  shape as **[the Forest Master](../wiki/npcs/the-forest-master.md)** of
  [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md).
- **[Renob](../wiki/characters/renob.md) kills one of the mud elementals.**
- **Boilerdang recasts Spirit Guardians** and moves next to the **second mud elemental** — which
  **dies** in the aura. Both elementals down.
- **The Heart swings at Boilerdang and misses**, spews **another poison cloud**, and **summons a
  replacement elemental.** *(It can keep making them — the elementals are not a finite force.)*
- **The Heart is worn down to roughly half** — the whole party contributing damage. Boilerdang
  sweeps his Spirit Guardians aura
  through the elemental and the Heart, and heals **five party members** with a 3rd-level
  **Mass Healing Word.**
- **The round ends with the party taking only minimal damage.** The fight has swung: the Heart
  at half, its adds stripped, nobody down.
- **💔 The water recedes and [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) is exposed — he
  appears to be dead.** He had been under since [Renob](../wiki/characters/renob.md) shoved him
  in.
- **⚔️ The Heart of the Swamp is killed** — Boilerdang simply **walks past it** and the Spirit
  Guardians aura finishes it off.
- **🕊️ Hederick was dying, not dead.** Boilerdang casts **Spare the Dying**, stabilising him.
- **🔚 Combat ends.** *(Loot to be posted — note the [wagons](../wiki/items/party-wagon.md) are
  cached in Darken Wood, so anything taken here is **carried**, not pooled.)*
- **🕊️ [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md) heals Hederick with the
  [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md)** — the High Theocrat of Solace, who burned holy symbols
  and called her god false, is raised up by the relic he confiscated.
- **🗝️ Hederick's reaction** (DM post, Crit Happens chat, 2026-08-11). He comes back with a
  violent gasp, half-submerged in black water, one leg trapped deep in the mud — coughing and
  clawing at the bank. Then he sees the dead Heart, and remembers:
  > *"That thing killed me."* … *"I was crushed by a swamp."* … *"I have spent my life
  > surrounded by idiots, heretics, goblins, and bureaucrats... and somehow **this** is how I
  > die?"* … *"A plant."* … *"Get me out of this filth."*

  Then, to whoever revived him:
  > *"And do not misunderstand my gratitude. I am grateful."* … *"I am also going to burn this
  > entire swamp down."*

  **Two things this establishes:** the Heart is a **plant creature** — "a ruined mass of roots
  and tendrils," which is why the first sight of it looked like moving roots — and
  **Hederick believes the swamp killed him.** He does not know
  [Renob](../wiki/characters/renob.md) shoved him in. He is also **stuck in the mud** and needs
  hauling out.
- **🗺️ The Heart's death thins the fog** — the swamp clears somewhat with the creature gone.
- **⚠️ A [draconian](../wiki/npcs/draconians.md) figure is seen in the distance**, disappearing
  into the fog. **The dragon army is already in the swamp** — and it watched the fight.
- **🐉 A DRAGON.** *(DM post, Crit Happens chat, 2026-08-11 — the campaign's first.)* At the far
  edge of the swamp, beyond the dead trees and drifting reeds, the fog shifts and something
  moves inside it. Not close — far enough that only **scale** registers. A vast shadow passes
  behind the mist, and for a few seconds the outline becomes clear:
  > *A long neck. A horned head. Two enormous wings spreading through the fog. A dragon-shaped
  > silhouette standing somewhere out in the swamp.*

  They never see its feet. Never hear a roar. Never see it move clearly. **But the shape turns,
  and for one awful moment it looks directly toward the island.** Then the fog rolls back
  across it. Gone.

  **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw.** On a failure: **frightened until the end of their next turn**,
  and **cannot willingly move toward the direction where the dragon appeared.** On a success:
  they keep control — but they still know exactly what they think they just saw.
  **Two nat 1s** — those characters will not want to proceed toward the dragon.
- **🕯️ The party takes a long rest on the island**, on the corpse of the Heart — with a
  draconian scout having withdrawn into the fog and a dragon somewhere out in the swamp.
  **The rest is safe and uninterrupted.** Nothing came for them: not the draconian that watched
  the fight, not the dragon that looked straight at the island.
- **🎁 Loot from the island is still to be posted** by the DM. When it arrives it goes into
  [the party inventory](../wiki/items/party-wagon.md) as **carried** gear — the wagons are
  cached back in Darken Wood.
- **🔚 Session ends** with the party rested on the seventh island, the road to Xak Tsaroth open
  ahead of them, and a dragon somewhere between here and there.

## New facts learned

- **The swamp had a keeper: the [Heart of the Swamp](../wiki/npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md)** —
  a huge **plant creature**, "a ruined mass of roots and tendrils," that used the whole seventh
  island as its body.
- **It was a *title*, not a species** — the same shape of name as
  **[the Forest Master](../wiki/npcs/the-forest-master.md)** of
  [Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md). Each wild place in this region appears to
  have a ruling spirit.
- **Its tentacles were separate HP pools**, roughly 85 each — bloodied by a single 43-damage
  hit. Killing them did not damage the body.
- **It was resistant to poison and acid**, spewed **poisoned-condition gas clouds** in a 20-ft
  radius, **summoned mud elementals at will**, and sent **water surges** that knocked the whole
  party prone. ([Heart of the Swamp](../wiki/npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md))
- **Radiant damage was the answer** — Spirit Guardians alone killed two tentacles, a mud
  elemental, and finally the Heart itself.
- **The Heart is what killed the dead Solamnic knight** on this island. *(Inference — it was
  sitting on the only road, killing what used it.)*
  ([Knights of Solamnia](../wiki/factions/knights-of-solamnia.md))
- **🗺️ Killing it thinned the fog.** The swamp crossing got easier — for the party, and for
  anyone else on the road. ([The Drowned Swamp](../wiki/locations/the-drowned-swamp.md))
- **[Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md) can use the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md) to heal
  deliberately** — the first time it has been called on rather than acting on its own.
- **[Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) died a third time and was raised by
  Mishakal's Staff** — and **said "I am grateful."** He believes **the swamp** killed him; he
  does not know [Renob](../wiki/characters/renob.md) shoved him in.
- **🐉 The campaign's first dragon.** A dragon-shaped silhouette standing out in the swamp —
  long neck, horned head, two enormous wings — that **turned and looked directly at the
  island** before the fog closed. No feet ever visible, no roar, no clear movement.
  ([The Dragon in the Fog](../wiki/npcs/the-dragon-in-the-fog.md))
- **The dragon army is already in the swamp.** A [draconian](../wiki/npcs/draconians.md) scout
  watched the fight and withdrew into the fog without engaging.
- **Nothing attacked during the long rest** — despite the scout and the dragon both knowing
  where the party was.

## Loose threads

- **🐉 What was the dragon?** Colour, allegiance, and whether it was even physically there —
  *"you never see its feet"*, and the DM's own wording is *what they think they just saw.*
- **Did it see them?** It looked directly at the island. If the draconian scout had already
  reported, the party's position, Goldmoon, and the Staff are all known.
- **Why did nothing come for them in eight hours?** A scout, a dragon, and an undefended
  exhausted party — and no attack. Being *watched* rather than hunted is its own answer.
- **⚠️ Did the party just open the road for the dragon army?** The Heart was killing everything
