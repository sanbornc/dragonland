## Recap

The party has reached **[Xak Tsaroth](../wiki/locations/xak-tsaroth.md)** — the site
the campaign has circled since it was known only as "X.T." Inside the ruin lie
**fallen statue pieces and toppled pillars**, and beyond them a **tomb**.

**[Renob "The Stabinator"](../wiki/characters/renob.md) crossed the gap by parkour.**
He launched himself across the fallen statuary and pillars and finished with a
**triple backflip into the tomb** — described at the table as an *amazing* act, and
**game- and mind-altering**. Flagged by the table as one of the important moments of the
campaign.
