# Session 05 — The Missing Stars

> **Session in progress (live log).** Camped in the woods outside Solace with their
> bound captive [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md), the party took a
> long rest — **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s Potion of
> Watchful Rest** letting them stay alert through it. A [Seeker](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) search party passed in the
> distance without closing in; then came **smoke.** [Malix](../wiki/characters/malix.md)
> flew up on his broom and confirmed **Solace is burning** — the party's own **HQ**
> among the flames — with a goblin search party below and, under **three moons**, two
> constellations **gone from the sky: the Queen of Darkness and the Dragon.** Too far
> out to reach their HQ, the party finished the rest, then **interrogated Hederick**,
> who revealed the staff-hunt and the goblins both come by order of **[Haven](../wiki/locations/haven.md)**
> — and let slip that the "allied" goblins have left his own priests **afraid to speak.**
> Haven's doctrine is commanded by **Lord Seeker Elistan** — and, Hederick warns, by
> healing him with the Staff the party has **marked him (and themselves) for death** as
> heretics in Elistan's eyes.

## Recap

- The party made camp in the woods outside [Solace](../wiki/locations/solace.md), the
  bound, twice-revived captive [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) in the
  [wagon](../wiki/items/party-wagon.md), and settled in for a **long rest.**
- **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) drank a Potion of Watchful
  Rest** — gaining the full benefit of the long rest while staying awake and alert on guard.
- **During the watch, a [Seeker](../wiki/factions/the-seekers.md) search party passed in
  the distance.** Goblins hunting the party — but they **did not close in.**
- **Then: smoke.** [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) caught it
  on the air and woke [Malix](../wiki/characters/malix.md) to investigate. The smoke seemed
  to be coming from the direction of Solace.
- **Malix flew up on his broom of flying** for an aerial look and saw:
  - A **goblin search party** on the ground below.
  - **Fire rising from Solace** — and it looked like the party's own **headquarters** in
    town was ablaze.
  - By the light of **three moons**, the stars were **wrong**: two constellations were
    **missing from the sky — the Queen of Darkness and the Dragon.**
- **The party is originally from Solace; their HQ is there — but they were too far out to
  reach it.** Hometown and base burning, out of reach, they **finished the long rest**
  (the watch held; the search party never returned).
- **On waking, the party interrogated [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md):**
  - **Why are the Seekers hunting the Staff?** — On **orders from [Haven](../wiki/locations/haven.md).**
    The staff-hunt is directed from Haven, not Hederick's own initiative.
  - **What about his own healing by the Staff?** — He dismissed it as **witchcraft**
    (consistent with his earlier "blasphemy" — doctrinally unmoved even as a prisoner).
  - **Where do the goblins come from?** — They **arrived as escorts and guards, sent from
    Haven as allies.**
  - **Hederick's tell:** *"...but allies do not make priests afraid to speak."* Even the
    fanatic High Theocrat senses the "allied" goblins have **cowed his own priests into
    silence** — the alliance reads far more like an occupation.
  - **Who commands Haven?** — **Lord Seeker Elistan**, who commands both Haven and the
    Seekers' **doctrine**. Hederick is his subordinate.
  - **The death-mark.** By **healing Hederick with the Staff**, [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)
    has **marked Hederick for death** under Haven's own doctrine — being cured by true /
    "witch" healing is itself a capital heresy. And Hederick warns the party: *if Elistan
    has marked you for death, **you will die.*** (This recasts Hederick's Session 04
    self-maiming and suicide leap: to a true believer, being healed by the Staff was not
    rescue but **damnation** — better to die clean than live marked.)
  - **The burning bothered him.** When [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)
    told Hederick it was the party's **HQ** (and the town) burning, Hederick was visibly
    **troubled** — he had been **assured the town would not be touched.** Watching Solace
    burn reads as a **broken promise** from the very "allies" he serves, wedging him
    further from his Haven masters.
  - **Why the Staff, really (Hederick's read):** because **its power and the miracles it
    works threaten the Seekers' power.** A relic that performs *genuine* healing disproves
    the Seekers' founding claim that true divine power is dead — so it's an existential
    threat to their legitimacy, and Haven wants it seized and silenced, not merely
    confiscated.
- **Reading the region.** The party studied a regional map (a DM aid — see
  [Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../wiki/documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md)):
  **[Solace](../wiki/locations/solace.md)** sits in the northeast; **[Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md)**
  lies between Solace and **[Haven](../wiki/locations/haven.md)** to the southwest;
  **[Que-Shu](../wiki/locations/que-shu.md)** — [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md) &
  [Riverwind](../wiki/npcs/riverwind.md)'s homeland — is just east of Solace; and the
  elven lands **[Qualinost](../wiki/locations/qualinost.md) / [Qualinesti](../wiki/locations/qualinesti.md)**
  lie to the south and west. **The road to Haven — source of the staff-hunt — runs south
  past Darken Wood.**
- **Into Darken Wood.** Heading south from Solace toward Haven, the party entered
  **[Darken Wood](../wiki/locations/darken-wood.md)** — a forest they **know to be haunted
  by undead.**
- **Encounter in the wood.** They came upon a party of **5 goblins, 1 human, and a
  cloaked figure.** The lone human among the goblins and the unidentified cloaked figure
  are the wildcards — this may be a hunting party sent after the now-marked PCs, a Seeker
  patrol, or something tied to Darken Wood itself. *(Unresolved — scene in progress.)*
- **Hederick fears the cloaked figure — and tells the party to run.** The captive
  [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) warned that the cloaked figure is
  **"not to be trifled with"** and urged the party to **flee.** A High Theocrat who takes
  his orders from Haven — and would know its agents on sight — is frightened enough to tell
  his own captors to run. That corroborates the cloaked figure (not the goblins) as the
  real danger, and hints it may be one of Haven's fearsome "allies" — the kind that leave
  priests afraid to speak. **Yet Hederick could not name it** — he fears it on instinct but
  does not recognize who or what it is, making it an *unknown* power rather than a familiar
  Haven official. *(Open question: genuine warning, or a ploy to spark a panicked flight
  Hederick could exploit to escape?)*

### The Darken Wood ambush (blow-by-blow)
- The cloaked figure was **nameless even to [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)**
  — feared on sight, unrecognized. The party chose to **attack**, opening with a **stealthy
  approach.**
- **[Pip](../wiki/characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) stealth-killed two goblins** — but the
  **second kill alerted the search party**, blowing the ambush.
- **[Malix](../wiki/characters/malix.md) dropped a Fireball** — **killed the remaining
  goblins** and **seriously wounded the human.** The **cloaked figure barely seemed hurt by
  the flames** — a first hint at its nature (**fire-resistant**).
- **[Vaelor](../wiki/characters/vaelor-grinward.md) summoned a giant elk as a steed** and
  rode it down on the human — the **elk gored the human to death.**
- **Vaelor then struck the cloaked figure with his axe — and it turned to stone**, the
  sudden petrification **trapping his axe** in the now-stone body. Whether the figure is
  inert, defending itself, or mid-transformation is unknown.
- **Then: three more appear.** Three cloaked creatures — **reptilian and winged** — stepped
  out, cloaked like the first, and addressed the party's cleric **by name:**
  **"Boilerdang, let's talk."** This reframes the slain figure: the stone-death is
  characteristic of these creatures, so the party has **already killed one of their kind**
  — and now three more offer to **parley**, not fight.
- **The match to prophecy.** Winged, scaled beings surfacing just as the **Dragon** left the
  night sky reads straight off the [Dragon Prophecy](../wiki/lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md):
  *"the scaled children of forgotten fire shall rise, the sky shall remember wings."* These
  may be the first of the prophecy's dragon-kin — and, plausibly, the fearsome "allies" out
  of [Haven](../wiki/locations/haven.md) that leave priests afraid to speak.
- **Their price: [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) and the
  [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md).** The three named their demand — hand over the captive
  theocrat and [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md)'s blue Staff. This **confirms them as the
  power behind the staff-hunt**: the entire campaign chase for the Staff (checkpoints, the
  door-to-door sweep, the burning of Solace) traces up to *these* creatures. It puts the party
  at a defining fork — the Staff is **not theirs to give** (it's Goldmoon's, and surrendering
  it betrays the [travelers](../wiki/npcs/the-two-travelers.md) they crossed the country to
  protect), while Hederick is **already marked for death by Haven**, so handing him over likely
  feeds him to that sentence.
- **"It was taken from us."** When the party protested that the Staff wasn't theirs to give,
  the creatures answered: *"that is where you are wrong — it was **taken from us**."* They
  **claim prior ownership** of the [Staff](../wiki/items/the-staff.md) — a provenance reveal
  that may tie to [Riverwind](../wiki/npcs/riverwind.md)'s quest **east, "at the lip of the
  morning,"** where he found the blue Staff ([Song of Riverwind](../wiki/documents/song-of-riverwind.md)).
  *(Their claim — not confirmed truth.)*
- **The forgery gambit.** Rather than surrender the real relic, the party **forged a fake
  staff** and brought it with Hederick to the handoff. The fake was **crudely made**, but the
  party landed a **strong Deception** — enough to make the trade happen.
- **The handoff turned bloody — and the ruse was caught.** On receiving the pair, the
  creatures **immediately stabbed [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)** (they
  wanted the marked theocrat **dead** — the death-mark carried out by *their* hand), then
  **inspected the staff, saw through the forgery,** and **combat resumed.** The party kept the
  *real* Staff (still with [Goldmoon](../wiki/npcs/goldmoon.md)) and offloaded Hederick — but
  bought only moments. The fight is back on.
- **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) cast Spirit Guardians** — his
  divine radiance **bloodied two** of the three creatures at once; the **third saved** (half
  damage, lightly hurt). Where Malix's Fireball did almost nothing, **true divine power bit
  deep** — and the aura slows them, screening Goldmoon and the Staff. The night's key
  discovery: the dragon-kin shrug off fire but **not** the very holy power Haven brands heresy.
  Killing them with the aura (spell damage) also sidesteps the weapon-trapping stone-death.
- **[Pip](../wiki/characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) shot the least-hurt creature dead** — a
  clean ranged kill (no trapped weapon). Of the three that appeared, **one down; two bloodied
  ones remain**, both caught in Boilerdang's Spirit Guardians.
- **Pip's second shot dropped another bloodied one** — two of the three dead; **one bloodied
  creature left.**
- **[Malix](../wiki/characters/malix.md) fumbled a Chromatic Orb — natural 1.** The spell
  misfired: he **took 1 damage** and **failed a Con save**, leaving him with **disadvantage on
  all spellcasting until the end of combat.** (Another Malix nat-1 Chromatic Orb mishap — cf.
  the exploding orb in Session 04. The bit continues.)
- **[Vaelor](../wiki/characters/vaelor-grinward.md)'s elk gored the last creature to death** —
  it **turned to stone on the elk's horn** (the same petrifying death that trapped Vaelor's
  axe). **All four dragon-kin are dead** (the first cloaked figure + the three), and the party
  **kept the real Staff.** The party left combat.
- **[Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) raised [Hederick](../wiki/npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)
  — again.** With the fight over, Boilerdang cast Revivify on the stabbed theocrat and healed
  him. Hederick is **alive once more — and furious**: the party had just **traded him to the
  very creatures who knifed him**, and now he's been dragged back yet again by the
  "blasphemous" divine power he despises. *(Tally: killed → revived → suicide → revived →
  stabbed by dragon-kin → revived. The man cannot stay dead, and hates every resurrection.)*
- **Looting the ambush, the party found a Seeker proclamation** — a **condemnation notice
  "By order of the Highseekers of Solace."** It declares a **property condemned as "a den of
  murderers, blasphemers, and enemies of the people,"** warns that any who **harbored, aided,
  or hid** the fugitives ("known to have slain lawful servants of the Seekers") will be
  **questioned, judged, or share their fate,** and frames the fire as a warning: *"No home,
  hall, shop, or family name will shield traitors from justice."* This is the decree behind the
  blaze at the party's **[Solace](../wiki/locations/solace.md) HQ** — the Seekers **made an
  example of the fugitives' home** and threaten **collective punishment** on anyone tied to
  them. Full text → [Highseekers' Condemnation Notice](../wiki/documents/highseekers-condemnation-notice.md)
  *(to transcribe at distillation).*
- **[Riverwind](../wiki/npcs/riverwind.md) recognized the creatures — but only as a distant
  memory.** As he drew closer he knew them from somewhere in his past, though the recollection
  is faint. This dovetails with the creatures' claim that the Staff *"was taken from us"* and
  with Riverwind's quest **east** for "strong magic" ([Song of Riverwind](../wiki/documents/song-of-riverwind.md))
  — his half-forgotten eastern journey may be where he first met **both** these creatures and
  the Staff.

## Loose threads

- **Who is Lord Seeker Elistan?** Now named as the commander of Haven and its doctrine —
  the head above Hederick and the true architect of the staff-hunt. How far does his reach
  extend, and can a death-mark be lifted or outrun?
- **The party is marked for death by Haven/Elistan** — especially [Boilerdang](../wiki/characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md),
  the visible miracle-worker. Being condemned by the Seekers' supreme authority raises the
  stakes on every town under Haven's reach.
- **Who commands the goblins at Haven?** The goblin "allies" trace to Haven, but a Seeker
  theocracy commanding a goblin army is a strange pairing — who really holds their leash?
- **The alliance is really an occupation.** If Haven's goblins have priests afraid to
