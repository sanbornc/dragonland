# Pale Blue Whetstone

Recovered from the sarcophagus in the lower chamber of
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**, alongside
**[the Silver Arm](the-silver-arm.md)** and
**[the funerary inscription tablet](../documents/funerary-inscription-tablet.md)**.

## What we know

Stat block as given by the DM, verbatim:

> A small whetstone of pale blue stone that never seems to dull. Once per long rest, using it
> on a weapon gives **+1 to the next damage roll** made with that weapon.

- **Once per long rest**, applied to a weapon. (Session 09)
- **+1 to the next damage roll** made with that weapon. (Session 09)
- **It never seems to dull.** (Session 09)
- Found in the sarcophagus of a smith remembered as
  **"He whose hand armed the world against wings."** (Session 09)
