# Dragon-Egg Shell Fragments

**Three dragon-egg shell fragments, wrapped in black cloth**, recovered from the camp at
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**. (Session 09)

## What we know

- **Three fragments**, kept **wrapped in black cloth** — someone was handling them
  deliberately, not hoarding rubble. (Session 09)
- **⚠️ One still has faint magical residue.** (Session 09)
- They match the **remnants of a giant egg shell** on the **dais** in the entry room,
  found in Session 08.
- **The egg itself is gone** — the
  [Dragon Army requisition order](../documents/dragon-army-requisition-order.md) proves it was
  **transported away under heavy guard.** (Session 09)

## Open questions

- **What is the residue, and what was done to the egg?**
- **Why were these wrapped in black cloth and kept** rather than shipped with the egg?
- **Whose egg was it?**
- **Are the fragments worth anything, or dangerous to carry?**
