# Stillwater

A **+1 longsword** recovered from the root-bed of
**[the Heart of the Swamp](../npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md)** after the party killed it.
(Session 07)

## What we know

- **+1 to attack and damage rolls.**
- **The blade is old but completely untouched by rust** — after years submerged in a drowned
  swamp, inside a creature that swallowed everything else down to bones and packs.
- **It has a name**, which most of the party's recovered weapons do not.

## Open questions

- **Whose was it?** A named, rust-proof blade in a swamp full of the Heart's victims — it had
  an owner who mattered. Is it connected to the **dead
  [Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md)** on the same island, or to the
  **[drowned adventurer](../documents/drowned-adventurers-journal.md)** under the roots?
- **Does "untouched by rust" mean more than flavour** — a property beyond the +1?
- **Unclaimed as of the end of Session 07.**
