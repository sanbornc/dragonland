# Scarab Charm

A **small carved scarab-like charm**, removed from the **antechamber** of
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**. (Session 09)

## What we know

Stat block as given by the DM, verbatim:

> Once per long rest, the wearer can add **1d4 to a failed Wisdom save**.

- **Worn**, not held. (Session 09)
- **Once per long rest**, **after** a Wisdom save fails. (Session 09)

## Open questions

- **Who wears it?** Party convention pools loot to the [party wagon](party-wagon.md) — **not
  assigned at the table yet.**
- **Does it need attunement?** Nothing recorded; the stat block as given does not say so.
