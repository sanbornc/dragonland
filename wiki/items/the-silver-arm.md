# The Silver Arm

Found in the sarcophagus in the lower chamber of
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**, where the
occupant's **right arm** should have been. The statues call it **"the silver hand."**

## What the statues said

- **[Pip](../characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) asked the crocodile statue: _"who is here
  entombed"_** — the answer, verbatim:

  > “Not the first. Only the silver hand remains.”

- **[Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) asked: _"what is the silver hand"_**
  — the **cat-headed statue** answered, verbatim:

  > “It is the hand that forged salvation.”

  **The cat then visibly relaxed from its attack stance**, and added:

  > “You see what was. You see what became. Understanding is enough”

  (Session 09)

## The sarcophagus, as described

Recorded **verbatim** from the DM. 🧩

> The stone lid shifts with a deep grinding scrape.
>
> A breath of air escapes from inside—dry, cold, and carrying the faint smell of old metal and
> long-dead incense.
>
> Your light falls into the sarcophagus.
>
> There is no body.
>
> Only the remains of one.
>
> Ancient linen lies collapsed into dust along the bottom, arranged in the shape of a man who
> has been gone for centuries. Small fragments of bone remain near the shoulders and ribs, but
> most of the original occupant is little more than powder.
>
> And resting where the right arm should be is something untouched by age.
>
> A silver arm.
>
> Not a gauntlet.
>
> Not armor.
>
> An entire arm, from shoulder to fingertips, forged in articulated plates of pale silver metal.
>
> The workmanship is extraordinary.
>
> Every joint is shaped with impossible precision. Fine lines run along the forearm like veins,
> disappearing beneath overlapping plates at the wrist and elbow. Tiny runes have been hammered
> into the metal so finely that they are almost invisible until the light catches them.

## What we know

- **An entire arm — shoulder to fingertips — in articulated plates of pale silver metal.**
  **Not a gauntlet. Not armor.** (Session 09)
- **It is untouched by age**, while the body it lay with has gone to powder. (Session 09)
- **It bears tiny runes**, hammered so finely they are almost invisible until light catches
  them. **Not yet read.** (Session 09)
- **The occupant of the sarcophagus is gone** — dust, linen, and fragments of bone near the
  shoulders and ribs. (Session 09)
- **The tomb is pre-Cataclysm; the sarcophagus is much newer**, per
  [the archaeologist](../npcs/the-archaeologist.md). (Session 09)

## Session 09 — taken, and swung

- **🥈 [Renob](../characters/renob.md) grabbed it** out of the sarcophagus. (Session 09)
- **⚔️ He walked to the crocodile statue and WHACKED THE SHIELD with it.** *(Which
  shield — the crocodile's or his own — is not recorded. **Outcome not yet
  recorded.**)* (Session 09)
- **🐊 The crocodile then attacked Renob — and missed**, leaving **its weapon lodged
  in the ground**, and said **“Strength without restraint becomes its own prison”**.
  (Session 09) 🧩
- **👊 Renob then “booped” the crocodile gently on the nose with it.** The
  crocodile said **“restraint is noted”**, stepped back and **powered down** —
  **ending the fight.** (Session 09)
- **⚒️ It belonged to a SMITH**, named on the
  [funerary tablet](../documents/funerary-inscription-tablet.md) only as **“He whose hand
  armed the world against wings.”** (Session 09)
- **Nothing is recorded about attunement, properties, or whether it can be worn.**
