# Mirewalker Ring

Recovered from the root-bed of **[the Heart of the Swamp](../npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md)**
after the party killed it. (Session 07)

## What it does

**Requires attunement.**

- **Ignore difficult terrain** caused by **mud, shallow water, roots, and swamp vegetation.**
- **Advantage on saving throws against being restrained by plants or mud.**
- **Once per long rest:** gain a **swim speed equal to your walking speed for 10 minutes.**

## Notes

- **It is the answer to the swamp the party is still standing in.** Everything it counters —
  mud, roots, plant restraint, being dragged into water — is exactly what nearly killed the
  party and did drown
  [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md). *(He was still stuck in the mud after being
  raised.)*
- **It came out of the thing it protects against**, which suggests a previous owner who was
  crossing this same swamp and was swallowed anyway.
- **Unclaimed as of the end of Session 07** — no attunement decided at the table.

## Open questions

- **Who attunes to it?** Only relevant while the party is in the swamp — but they are still
  islands away from [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md), and Xak Tsaroth is a **drowned
  ruin.**
