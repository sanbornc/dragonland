## What we know
- **True divine power is believed to have vanished centuries ago.** Most people
  in the world think the gods no longer answer. (GM background — [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md))
- **Mundane healing still exists** — through herbs, skill, rest, potions, and
  rare arcane magic. What is missing is **true holy healing**: wounds closing
  through prayer, disease lifting by divine grace, life returning because a god
  *answered*. Most people have never seen it. (GM background)
- **Real miracles are dangerous.** Because true holy power is so rare, those who
  display it provoke extreme reactions — some would worship it, some would fear
  it, and some would call it fraud, witchcraft, or heresy. (GM background)
- **False priests and Seekers** still claim religious authority, but they cannot
  perform real miracles. The "Seekers" appear to be a recognized religious
  authority/order in the world. (GM background)
- A person whose prayers genuinely *answer* is, in the world's terms, a true
  **[miracle-bearer](miracle-bearers.md)** — which is exactly the rare and
  dangerous thing the [Ringmaster](../npcs/the-ringmaster.md) traffics as
  "special cargo." This connects the religious lore to the tension the ledger
  records between "old gods" and "new lies."
- **[Boilerdang Criddlycrotch](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)** is a
  living example: as the campaign begins his prayers stop feeling empty and his
  divine magic works — almost unheard of in this world. (GM background)

- **⭐ The premise is inverted (Session 06).** The **[Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)**
  — a unicorn ruling [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md) — told the party the
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) is **"a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people;
  the people have forgotten the gods,"** made by **[Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md)**. The
  silence was never the gods'. What the world calls the death of divine power was the world
  turning away.
- **🕯️ A god is speaking now, not merely leaving relics.** The Forest Master's knowledge of
  the [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md) came from **a voice that spoke
  directly from her.** (Session 06)
- **🕊️ Absolution beat steel.** [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) ended
  a **three-century haunting** by absolving **[Emrich](../npcs/emrich.md)** — priest's work,
  succeeding where ~50 knights fighting endlessly had not. Divine power in this world is not
  only rare; it is **effective in ways nothing else is.** (Session 06)
- **The old order is still standing.** In one night the party met a **Solamnic company still
  keeping faith** and a **unicorn** — both of the pre-Cataclysm order the
  [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md) insist is gone. (Session 06)

## Open questions
- **Is [Mishakal](mishakal.md) the god who answers Boilerdang** — and is hers the voice that
  spoke through the Forest Master? (Session 06)
- Who or what are the **[Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)**, and what authority
  do they hold? Are they the "new lies" the Ringmaster's ledger alludes to? *(We now
  know they have an armed, goblin-staffed presence controlling entry to Solace —
  Session 03.)*
- *Why* has divine power gone silent — and why is it answering Boilerdang now?
- Is Boilerdang's power tied to a specific god, an "old spirit," or something else?
