# Mishakal

## What we know

- A **named god** in the world. The party recovered a **cheap wooden symbol of
  Mishakal** from the Seeker roadblock's lock box, **labeled "false god token."**
  (Session 03)
- The **[Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md) explicitly reject Mishakal as false**
  — consistent with their stance that true divine power is gone and all claimed holy
  power is fraud. See [Divine power in the world](../lore/divine-power.md).

- **⭐ Mishakal is a goddess — and the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) is her promise.** The
  **[Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)** of [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md)
  told the party the Staff is **not merely a weapon or a vessel of healing** but
  **"a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people; the people have forgotten the
  gods"** — and named **Mishakal** as the one who made it. (Session 06)
- **She is associated with healing.** Everything observed of her Staff fits: it **heals by
  touch**, and it flared blue to carry [Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md) and
  [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) out of a stoning. (Sessions 04–06)
- **[Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md) is the Staff's rightful bearer**, and the
  **[draconians](../npcs/draconians.md)' claim that it "was taken from us" is false** —
  stated flatly by the Forest Master. (Session 06)
- **Her relics include the [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md)**, at
  **[Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md)** — the party's current objective, at the
  Forest Master's direction. (Session 06)
- **🕯️ Something spoke through the Forest Master.** Her knowledge of the Disks came from
  **a voice that spoke directly from her** — evidence that a god is **actively speaking into
  the world now**, not merely leaving relics behind. *(Presumed Mishakal — it is her Staff,
  her Disks, her promise — but she did not name the voice.)* (Session 06)
- **The [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) is collecting Mishakal's relics.**
  Revealed when the party showed the Forest Master the confiscated token. Strip away the
  proof and the [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)' claim that the gods are gone becomes
  true by default. (Session 06)
- **The irony of the token.** The Seekers confiscated a cheap **wooden symbol of Mishakal**
  and labelled it a **"false god token"** (Session 03) — and the party carried it as junk
  loot in the [wagon](../items/party-wagon.md) for three sessions. They have been branding
  the *true* goddess false while hunting her relic across the region.

## Open questions

- Is Mishakal connected to **[Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s**
  real divine power — "a god whose name he is only beginning to recognize"? **Is hers the
  voice that answers him?** (Session 06)
- **Whose voice spoke through the [Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)?** Presumed
  Mishakal, unconfirmed. (Session 06)
- **What are the [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md)** and what do they
  do? (Session 06)
- Who else in the region worships Mishakal? (The token was among *confiscated* holy items,
  so someone does.)
- The party also holds a **Silver Holy Symbol of Paladine** (party inventory) —
  another named god. How do Mishakal, Paladine, and the "old gods" relate? The vanished
  constellations are said to **represent the old gods**. (Session 06)
