## What we know
- A brittle parchment recovered from the buried archive foretells the **return of dragons** — transcribed in [The Dragon Prophecy (Brittle Parchment)](../documents/dragon-prophecy-parchment.md). (session 02)
- Its signs read like the present age: **"the old faith is ash"** (collapsed religion), **"crowns break and oaths are hollow"** (failing kingdoms, broken oaths), and **"iron will march beneath black banners"** — explicitly naming the **Black Banners.** (session 02)
- The payoff: **"the scaled children of forgotten fire shall rise,"** **"the sky shall remember wings,"** **"the world shall learn again to fear dragons."** — the campaign's titular dragons returning. (session 02)
- **🐉 The prophecy is manifesting (Session 05).** Two events landed together in
  [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md): the **Dragon constellation vanished from the
  night sky** (seen under three moons, alongside the Queen of Darkness), and winged,
  reptilian **[draconians](../npcs/draconians.md)** stepped out of the wood and fought the
  party. The **"scaled children of forgotten fire" = draconians**, now walking the world as
  the **sky "remembers wings."** The prophecy's *"iron will march beneath black banners"* is
  the same army — the draconians serve the **[Black Banners](../factions/black-banners.md)**,
  carrying black-claw shields and Draconic order-tokens. (Session 05)

## Why it matters
- It ties the **Black Banners' war** to a draconic prophecy — their march may be a sign of, a cause of, or a servant to the dragons' return. ([Black Banners](../factions/black-banners.md))
- It frames the setting: a post-faith, post-Cataclysm world on the edge of a return of dragons.

## Open questions
- "The old faith" may be the worship of **Paladine** — a silver holy symbol of Paladine was recovered at the site. (session 02)
- Are the Black Banners trying to **fulfil** the prophecy, **prevent** it, or unknowingly **enacting** it?
- ~~Who are the "scaled children of forgotten fire" — true dragons, draconians, or something else?~~ **Answered Session 05: [draconians](../npcs/draconians.md)** — winged, reptilian, cloaked creatures that serve the Black Banners and die into stone. Whether *true* dragons follow is still open.
- Is the buried archive's hidden knowledge connected to the prophecy?
- The **Queen of Darkness** constellation vanished alongside the Dragon — is her return (a dark god?) bound up with the draconians' rise? (Session 05)
