# Regional Map: Solace–Haven

> *A regional map provided by the DM as a player aid, first used in-play in
> [Session 05](../../sessions/05-2026-07-07-the-missing-stars.md).*

A weathered regional map showing the lands around **[Solace](../locations/solace.md)**
and **[Haven](../locations/haven.md)**, with dashed roads linking the settlements,
scattered hills, and forest. Posted by the DM (Jeremy Maughan) in the group's
"Crit Happens" chat on **2026-07-07** and studied by the party this session.

## Legible labels (west → east)

- **[Qualinesti](../locations/qualinesti.md)** — a large vertical label running down
  the **western edge** of the map; an elven realm.
- **[Qualinost](../locations/qualinost.md)** — a city in the **south-center**, marked
  with a **ringed / target symbol** (a concentric-circle glyph).
- **[Haven](../locations/haven.md)** — **center-left.**
- **[Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md)** — a forest in the **center**, sitting
  **between Haven and Solace.**
- **[Solace](../locations/solace.md)** — **upper right** (northeast).
- **[Que-Shu](../locations/que-shu.md)** (rendered "Que-sha" on the map) — just
  **east / south-east of Solace**; the plains homeland of the Que-Shu people
  ([Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md) & [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md)).

## Roads

- **Dashed roads** link the settlements. A road runs **south from Solace toward Haven,
  passing Darken Wood** — the route the party is taking toward Haven (the source of the
  staff-hunt, per [Session 05](../../sessions/05-2026-07-07-the-missing-stars.md)).

## Illegible / uncertain (in-fiction)

- A partial label **"Kas…"** runs off the **top edge above Solace** — the rest is
  cut off by the torn edge, so the full name is unknown. *(Uncertain.)*
- There appears to be a **second vertical label on the far west, below/beside
  "Qualinesti,"** but it is not clearly legible — noted here rather than guessed.
  *(Uncertain.)*
- The far **right edge** is cropped, and minor labels there are cut off.
