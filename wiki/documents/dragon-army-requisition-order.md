# Dragon Army Requisition Order

Recovered from the camp at
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)** after the fight.
(Session 09)

## What it proves

> **The egg was transported away under heavy guard.**

- **⚠️ There WAS an egg**, and **the [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) took it.**
  (Session 09)
- **It left under HEAVY GUARD** — they treated it as high-value cargo, not loot. (Session 09)
- This is the **dais in the entry room**, where the party found only **the remnants of a giant
  egg shell** in Session 08. **The shell is what was left behind.** (Sessions 08–09)
- The party also recovered **three dragon-egg shell fragments wrapped in black cloth**, one
  with **faint magical residue** — see
  [Dragon-Egg Shell Fragments](../items/dragon-egg-shell-fragments.md). (Session 09)

## Open questions

- **Where was the egg taken, and is it still intact?**
- **Whose egg, and of what?** Nothing establishes the species or parentage.
- **Is this why the dragon army sacked this tomb at all** — was the egg the prize, and the
  looting incidental?
- **Does it connect to [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md)** and the
  [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md), two days on?
- **Who wrote and signed the order?** Not recorded.
