# Funerary Inscription Tablet

A **short funerary inscription tablet** recovered from the sarcophagus in the lower chamber of
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**, alongside
**[the Silver Arm](../items/the-silver-arm.md)** and the
**[pale blue whetstone](../items/pale-blue-whetstone.md)**.

## Transcription

It **names the smith only indirectly**:

> **“He whose hand armed the world against wings.”**

*(That is the whole of what is recorded. Nothing else on the tablet is logged, and **the smith
is never named.**)*

## What this tells us

- **The occupant of the sarcophagus was a SMITH.** (Session 09)
- **He is remembered for arming the world against "wings"** — and in this campaign, the thing
  with wings is **dragons**. See [The Dragon Prophecy](../lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md) and
  [Black Banners](../factions/black-banners.md). *(The reading of "wings" as dragons is an
  inference; the tablet says "wings.")*
- **His name is deliberately withheld** — the inscription refers to him only by deed.
- **The statues called his arm "the hand that forged salvation."** (Session 09)
- **The crocodile statue said of him: "Not the first. Only the silver hand remains."**
  (Session 09)

## Open questions

- **Who was he?** The inscription avoids naming him. **Why?**
- **"Not the first"** — not the first smith, not the first burial, not the first to be armed?
- **What did he forge?** "Armed the world against wings" implies **weapons**, plural, and a
  **war** — not a single relic.
- **Does any of it survive**, and is the [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) looking
  for it? They sacked this tomb already.
- **Why is his much-newer burial inside a pre-Cataclysm tomb?**
