# Black Stone Tablet Fragment

A **fragment of black stone tablet** recovered from the camp at
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)**. (Session 09)

## What it shows

> **An ancient figure holding a long spear toward a dragon.**

- That is the **whole of what is recorded.** The figure is **not named**, the dragon is **not
  identified**, and **nothing on the fragment is confirmed.** (Session 09)
- It is a **fragment** — whatever else the tablet showed is missing.

## Open questions

- **Who is the figure?**
- **What is the long spear?** *(Undescribed beyond "a long spear.")*
- **Is this the same tradition as the two fighting dragons carved on the golden doors**, or a
  different, older one? The tomb already contains **Solamnic writing that does not match the
  rest of its markings.**
- **Where is the rest of the tablet?**
