# Highseekers' Condemnation Notice

A [Seeker](../factions/the-seekers.md) proclamation **looted from the draconian /
goblin ambush party in [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md)** (Session 05) —
likely carried by the lone **human courier** among the goblins. It is the decree
behind the **burning of the party's [Solace](../locations/solace.md) headquarters.**
Player-transcribed — no image.

## Transcription (verbatim)

> By order of the Highseekers of Solace:
>
> This property is condemned as a den of murderers, blasphemers, and enemies of the people.
>
> Those who harbored the fugitives known to have slain lawful servants of the Seekers will be questioned. Those who aided them will be judged. Those who hide them will share their fate.
>
> Let this fire stand as warning:
>
> No home, hall, shop, or family name will shield traitors from justice.

## What it tells us

- **The party are publicly branded fugitives.** They are named (in effect) as the
  "fugitives known to have slain lawful servants of the Seekers" — the roadblock
  crew (Session 03) and the inn brawl (Session 04) catching up with them.
- **Collective punishment is threatened against anyone who helps them.** Those who
  **harbored** them will be *questioned*, those who **aided** them *judged*, those who
  **hide** them will *share their fate* — "no home, hall, shop, or family name" is
  spared. Aimed squarely at the party's **own people in their hometown of Solace.**
- **A new authority is named: the "Highseekers of Solace."** The body issuing the
  condemnation — above or alongside [High Theocrat Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)
  in the [Seeker](../factions/the-seekers.md) hierarchy.
- **This is the decree behind the burning of the party's HQ.** The notice stands the
  fire up as a deliberate **warning** — the Seekers **made an example of the fugitives'
  home**, not a random blaze. Confirms what [Malix](../characters/malix.md) saw from the
  broom: Solace and the party's headquarters ablaze. (This sits uneasily against
  Hederick's belief that he was **"assured the town would not be touched"** — targeted
  at the fugitives specifically, or a broken promise from Haven's masters.)
- **It was in the hands of the draconian hunting party.** The condemnation was being
  carried by the [draconian](../npcs/draconians.md) / goblin unit tracking the party —
  linking the Solace crackdown, the fugitive-hunt, and the draconian trackers into one
  coordinated operation.

## Open questions

- Who exactly are the **"Highseekers of Solace"** — a council, a rank, or a title for
  those above Hederick? How do they relate to **Haven / [Elistan](../npcs/elistan.md)?**
- Whose homes and families in Solace are now in the crosshairs — the party is *from*
  Solace, so who did they leave behind?
