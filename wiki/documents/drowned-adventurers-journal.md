# The Drowned Adventurer's Journal

Recovered from **beneath the oldest roots** of
**[the Heart of the Swamp](../npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md)** after the party killed it —
still clutched in the hands of **a dead adventurer** the creature had swallowed.
(Session 07)

## Condition

**Waterproof, and still mostly ruined.** Only **one page** survives legibly: a **rough drawing
of a huge dragon-shaped silhouette hidden in heavy fog**, with a line written underneath it.

No name, no date, no other legible entry. Nothing else was recorded from the body.

## Transcription

> ***"It isn't a dragon. Whatever you see in the mist—do not follow it."***

## What this tells us

- **Someone came this way before and saw the same thing.** The party sighted a
  **[dragon-shaped silhouette in the fog](../npcs/the-dragon-in-the-fog.md)** minutes after
  killing the Heart — long neck, horned head, two vast wings, standing out in the swamp. This
  page is a drawing of the same shape, made by someone who died in this swamp.
- **⚠️ "It isn't a dragon."** Written by a witness, flatly, as the correction of a mistake.
  It matches what the party themselves never saw: **no feet, no roar, no clear movement** —
  and the DM's framing that they know *what they think they just saw.*
- **"Do not follow it."** The warning is not *do not fight it* or *do not approach it* — it is
  **do not follow.** Which implies the thing **leads**, and that following it is the failure
  mode. Whatever it is, it may work by drawing travellers off the causeway.
- **The writer still died here** — swallowed by the Heart, not by the shape in the mist.
  Whether they followed it first is unknown.
- **Three bodies on this road now:** the dead
  [Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) on the seventh island, this
  adventurer under the roots, and whatever else the Heart had swallowed over the years. The
  causeway to [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md) is well-travelled and badly survived.

## 🗝️ Session 09 — the warning is confirmed

**[Malix](../characters/malix.md) heard goblins INSIDE the dragon at
[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md), struggling to
control it**, and the dragon **took no action on its turn.** Whoever wrote *"It isn't a
dragon"* was **right** — the thing is crewed from the inside. See
[The Dragon in the Fog](../npcs/the-dragon-in-the-fog.md). (Session 09)

*(Whether the tomb's machine is the same silhouette this writer drew in the fog is still
**not** established.)*

## Open questions

- **Who was the adventurer**, and were they going to Xak Tsaroth too?
- **If it isn't a dragon, what is it?** A lure, an illusion, a projection, something wearing a
  dragon's shape — or something the writer simply couldn't name.
- **Does the warning still hold now that the Heart is dead**, or was the shape the Heart's
  doing all along? *(The party saw it **after** the Heart died, which argues against.)*
- **Did the writer follow it?**
