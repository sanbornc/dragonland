# The Silent Gods & the Seekers

**Status: active.** In a world that believes true divine power died centuries ago,
**[Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s prayers genuinely
answer** — and the ruling **[Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)** brand all such
power fraud or heresy. The party has just openly used it against them.

## The thread
- **Most people believe true divine power vanished centuries ago.** Mundane healing exists (herbs, potions, arcane), but **true holy miracles** are unheard of — and dangerous to display: worshipped by some, feared by others, condemned as fraud/witchcraft/heresy by the rest. ([Divine power in the world](../lore/divine-power.md), GM background)
- **Boilerdang is a true [miracle-bearer](../lore/miracle-bearers.md):** his divine magic works, for reasons even he doesn't understand — faith, an old spirit, or "a god whose name he is only beginning to recognize." (GM background)
- The **Seekers** are a theocratic authority under **[High Theocrat Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md)**, controlling [Solace](../locations/solace.md) by checkpoint. They **confiscate holy items, hunt "false holy symbols" and "fraudulent healing,"** and brand **[Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md)** a "false god." ([Notice of Road Authority](../documents/notice-of-road-authority.md), Session 03)
- Named gods are surfacing: **Mishakal** (Seekers call false) and **Paladine** (a silver holy symbol of Paladine was recovered; the prophecy's "old faith" may be his worship). ([The Dragon Prophecy](../lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md))
- At the Solace roadblock, **Boilerdang openly cast Spirit Guardians** against Seeker agents — a public display of real divine power. For now it's **contained** (no survivors; scene staged as infighting). (Session 03)
- **The doctrine has a head: [Lord Seeker Elistan](../npcs/elistan.md).** Under interrogation (Session 05), [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) named Elistan as commander of **[Haven](../locations/haven.md)** and of the Seekers' **doctrine** — his superior, above him. Elistan's doctrine **condemns those healed by true divine power** as a **capital heresy** (a death-mark): by curing Hederick with the Staff, Boilerdang marked *him* — and, Hederick warns, the whole party. It explains Hederick's Session 04 suicide (to a believer, being healed was **damnation**). (Session 05)
- **The gods may be *returning* — the sky is changing.** On the night of Session 05, under **three moons**, two constellations **vanished** — the **Dragon** and the **Queen of Darkness** — just as winged **[draconians](../npcs/draconians.md)** walked out of [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md). A sky-omen that reframes the "silent gods": powers may be **descending into the world.** This dovetails with Boilerdang's prayers newly answering — and with his **radiant power proving effective against the draconians** where fire failed (the light answers the dark). (Session 05) — see [The Dragon Prophecy](../lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md)

- **⭐ The doctrine is inverted (Session 06).** The **[Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)**
  — a **unicorn** ruling [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md) — told the party the
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) is **"a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people;
  the people have forgotten the gods,"** and named **[Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md)** as its
  maker. The Seekers' founding claim — that the gods died or abandoned the world at the
  Cataclysm — is backwards. **The silence was never the gods'.**
- **🕯️ A god is speaking *now*.** The Forest Master's knowledge of the
  **[Disks of Mishakal](the-disks-of-mishakal.md)** came from **a voice that spoke directly
  from her** — she is a conduit. Not a relic left behind: an active voice. *(Presumed
  Mishakal; unconfirmed.)* (Session 06)
- **🕊️ Divine power did what steel could not.** In Darken Wood,
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) ended a **three-century haunting**
  by **absolving [Emrich](../npcs/emrich.md)**, a Knight of Solamnia looping at the moment of
  the Cataclysm — releasing ~50 knights and dispelling the shadow figures that no amount of
  fighting had beaten. A priest's work, in a world that says priests are frauds. (Session 06)
- **The old order is not gone.** In one night the party met a **Solamnic company still keeping
  faith** and a **unicorn** presiding over a wood — both of the pre-Cataclysm order the
  Seekers insist died. (Session 06)
- **🔥 The doctrine is cracking from the inside: [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md).**
  Present for all of it — the absolution, the unicorn, and the naming of Mishakal as true —
  the captive High Theocrat's beliefs are **shaking**, and he chose to travel on with the
  party willingly. A **recanting High Theocrat** would be the most damaging possible witness
  against [Elistan](../npcs/elistan.md)'s doctrine. (Session 06)
- **The [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) is collecting Mishakal's relics.** Strip
  away the proof and the Seekers' story becomes true by default — which makes the race to
  [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md) a fight over **evidence**, not treasure.
  (Session 06)

## Open questions
- **Whose voice speaks through the [Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)** — and is it
  the same one that answers [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)? (Session 06)
- **Is "[the Queen](../npcs/the-queen.md)" a god?** If the vanished *Queen of Darkness* is an
  old god entering the world, the gathering war may be a god's war. *(Inference, Session 06.)*
- **Who and what are the Seekers** — and is Hederick their supreme leader or one theocrat among many? Are they the **"new lies"** the Ringmaster's ledger sets against the "old gods"?
- **Why did divine power go silent — and why is it answering Boilerdang now?** Is his power tied to a specific god (Mishakal? Paladine?), an old spirit, or something else?
- How do **Mishakal, Paladine, and the "old gods"** relate — and do they tie to **[Goldmoon's Staff](the-staff-and-goldmoon.md)** (a Que-Shu relic the Seekers want priests kept away from)?
- Is the return of the gods bound up with the foretold **[return of dragons](../lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md)**?
- **Why does the party dislike Hederick** — past history, or just his manner? What do the travelers want *from* him?
