# The Disks of Mishakal

**Status: active — the party's current objective.** The
[Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md) of [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md)
sent the party east to **[Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md)** to find the **Disks of
Mishakal** — and warned that the **dragon army is racing them for the same relics.**

## The thread

- **The Forest Master named the destination and the prize.** She directed the party to
  **travel to Xak Tsaroth and find the Disks of Mishakal** — revealing in the same breath
  that the campaign's long-unspoken **"[X.T.](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md)"** *is* Xak
  Tsaroth. (Session 06)
- **Her knowledge came from a voice that spoke directly from her** — she was *told*. She is
  a conduit for something. *(Presumed [Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md); not confirmed.)*
  (Session 06)
- **Why it matters.** The Forest Master describes the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) as
  **"a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people; the people have forgotten the
  gods,"** and names **Mishakal** as the promise's maker. The Disks are relics of the same
  goddess. (Session 06)
- **⚠️ It is a race.** Shown the confiscated Mishakal token, the Forest Master revealed the
  **dragon army is seeking those relics as well.** The
  [draconians](../npcs/draconians.md) are not just hunting the Staff — they are collecting
  **Mishakal's** things wholesale. (Session 06)
- **The road.** Xak Tsaroth is **protected by a great evil**; the **direct road is drowned**;
  the approach is **through a swamp**, on **stone roads beneath the water.** The Forest
  Master **gave the party maps.** (Session 06)
- **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) leads.** He **remembers a swamp** from the eastern
  quest on which he found the Staff — strong indication he has been to Xak Tsaroth before.
  He guides the party in. (Session 06)
- **⚠️ Direct conflict with the ledger.** The **[buyer](../npcs/the-buyer.md)** insisted —
  twice — that **"the Staff must not reach X.T."** The party is now taking the Staff's
  rightful bearer straight there. Someone with money and reach has worked since before this
  campaign began to prevent exactly this.
- **The party travels light.** They left the [wagons](../items/party-wagon.md) — and the
  coin, documents, and bulk gear in them — in Darken Wood under the Forest Master's
  protection, carrying only the Staff, the
  [Amulet of the Remembered Paw](../items/amulet-of-the-remembered-paw.md), the Mishakal
  token, the maps, the Silver Holy Symbol of Paladine, and supplies.
  **[Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) chose to come along.** (Session 06)
- **They carry a boon.** The Forest Master's blessing (weapon +1 / extra 4th-level slots /
  extra radiant Divine Smite) lasts **until the battle with the evil at Xak Tsaroth** — it
  gets them there, and then expires. (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **What are the Disks of Mishakal, and what do they do?** Never described.
- **What is the great evil guarding Xak Tsaroth** — the dragon army's, or older?
- **How far ahead is the dragon army?**
- **Why did the buyer want the Staff kept away from here?** Enemy, or someone who knows
  something the party doesn't?
- **Has [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) been here before** — is Xak Tsaroth "the lip of the
  morning," where he found the Staff and half-remembers draconians?
- **Whose voice speaks through the Forest Master** — and is it the one answering
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)?
- Does reaching the Disks with a recanting **High Theocrat** in tow give the party the
  evidence to break the [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)' doctrine at
  [Haven](../locations/haven.md)?
