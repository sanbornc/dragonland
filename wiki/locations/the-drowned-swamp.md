# The Drowned Swamp

The swamp east of [Darken Wood](darken-wood.md) that has swallowed the direct road to
**[Xak Tsaroth](xak-tsaroth.md)**. The party is crossing it as of the end of Session 06.

## What we know

- **The direct road to Xak Tsaroth is drowned**; the swamp is the only approach.
  ([Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md), Session 06)
- **There are stone roads beneath the water** — a submerged causeway that carries travellers
  through, **if they know where it lies.** The Forest Master **gave the party maps.**
  (Session 06)
- **The crossing runs island to island**, and **each island requires a saving throw** to cross
  safely. The party **made every save through seven islands.** (Session 06)
- **The fourth island is the source of the fog** — and the fog comes off the **refuse on the
  ground**: rotting cabbage, trash, and debris **emanating fog** on their own. Household
  rubbish, in a drowned swamp. *(Left uninvestigated by the party.)* (Session 06)
- **The seventh island is larger, and the fog is thicker there.** (Session 06)
- **A dead [Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) lies on the seventh
  island** with a sword and a **Solamnic-crested shield** — untouched, nothing else on the
  body. The party took the weapons;
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) gave him **last rites.** (Session 06)
- **⚠️ The seventh island was not an island.** As the party stood on it, **ripples moved in the
  water**, the **ground contracted**, and a **tendril** rose. It was the body of
  **[the Heart of the Swamp](../npcs/the-heart-of-the-swamp.md)** — a huge **plant creature**
  whose "roots" beneath the water were tentacles. The party **killed it** (Session 07).
- **🗺️ The fog thinned when the Heart died.** The mist was part of the creature, or its doing;
  the crossing became easier the moment it was dead — **for the party, and for anyone else on
  the road.** (Session 07)
- **⚠️ The [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) is already in the swamp.** A
  [draconian](../npcs/draconians.md) scout watched the fight from the fog and **withdrew
  without engaging.** (Session 07)
- **🐉 A dragon stands somewhere out in the swamp.** Seen at a distance as a silhouette in the
  fog — long neck, horned head, two enormous wings — which **turned and looked directly at the
  island** before the mist closed over it. See
  [The Dragon in the Fog](../npcs/the-dragon-in-the-fog.md). (Session 07)
- **The party took a safe, uninterrupted long rest on the seventh island**, on the Heart's
  corpse — despite the scout and the dragon both knowing where they were. **Nothing came.**
  (Session 07)
- **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) remembers a swamp** from his eastern quest, the journey
  on which he found the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md). He is **guiding the party through.**
  (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **⚠️ Did killing the Heart open the road for the dragon army?** It sat on the only causeway,
  killing what used it — including, most likely, the dead Solamnic. Now it doesn't.
- **What is the dragon**, and is it between the party and
  [Xak Tsaroth](xak-tsaroth.md)? ([The Dragon in the Fog](../npcs/the-dragon-in-the-fog.md))
- **Why was the long rest undisturbed?** Being *watched* rather than hunted is its own answer.
- **What made the refuse on the fourth island produce fog** — the Heart, or something else
  living out here? Is that fog gone too? Who eats and discards food in a drowned swamp?
- **Are the island saving throws against the fog**, the swamp, or were they the Heart's doing?
- **Who was the dead Solamnic, and what brought him toward Xak Tsaroth?**
- **Is this the swamp Riverwind crossed** — and does the causeway lead where he went before?
- How many islands is the crossing? **Seven so far.**
