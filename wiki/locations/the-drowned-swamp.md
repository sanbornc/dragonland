# The Drowned Swamp

The swamp east of [Darken Wood](darken-wood.md) that has swallowed the direct road to
**[Xak Tsaroth](xak-tsaroth.md)**. The party is crossing it as of the end of Session 06.

## What we know

- **The direct road to Xak Tsaroth is drowned**; the swamp is the only approach.
  ([Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md), Session 06)
- **There are stone roads beneath the water** — a submerged causeway that carries travellers
  through, **if they know where it lies.** The Forest Master **gave the party maps.**
  (Session 06)
- **The crossing runs island to island**, and **each island requires a saving throw** to cross
  safely. The party **made every save through seven islands.** (Session 06)
- **The fourth island is the source of the fog** — and the fog comes off the **refuse on the
  ground**: rotting cabbage, trash, and debris **emanating fog** on their own. Household
  rubbish, in a drowned swamp. *(Left uninvestigated by the party.)* (Session 06)
- **The seventh island is larger, and the fog is thicker there.** (Session 06)
- **A dead [Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md) lies on the seventh
  island** with a sword and a **Solamnic-crested shield** — untouched, nothing else on the
  body. The party took the weapons;
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) gave him **last rites.** (Session 06)
- **⚠️ The seventh island is not an island.** As the party stood on it, **ripples moved in the
  water**, the **ground contracted**, and a **tendril** rose. *(Session 06 ended here —
  Session 07 opens on initiative.)*
- **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) remembers a swamp** from his eastern quest, the journey
  on which he found the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md). He is **guiding the party through.**
  (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **What is the creature the party is standing on?**
- **What is the fog, and what makes the refuse on the fourth island produce it?** Who eats and
  discards food out here — is there a settlement, a lair, or something buried under the pile?
- **Are the island saving throws against the fog**, the swamp, or the creature?
- **Who was the dead Solamnic, and what brought him toward Xak Tsaroth?**
- **Is this the swamp Riverwind crossed** — and does the causeway lead where he went before?
- How many islands is the crossing?
