## What we know

- An **elven realm** running down the **western edge of the region**, labeled by a large
  vertical name along that edge.
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- Contains the elven city of **[Qualinost](qualinost.md)** (south-center, marked with a
  ringed symbol). ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- Lies **west / south-west** of [Haven](haven.md) and [Solace](solace.md), across the
  region from the Solace–Haven road.
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)

## Open questions

- There appears to be a **second, illegible vertical label** on the far west near
  Qualinesti on the map — its name and meaning are unknown.
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md))
- What are the elves of Qualinesti to the party's current troubles — the staff-hunt,
  [Haven](haven.md), and the [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)?
