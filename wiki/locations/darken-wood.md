## What we know

- A **forest in the center of the region**, lying **between [Solace](solace.md) and
  [Haven](haven.md).** ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- The **road running south from Solace toward Haven passes through / past Darken Wood.**
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- **The party knows it to be haunted by undead.** (Session 05)
- As of Session 05 the party is **entering it from the north** (just south of Solace),
  heading south toward Haven — the source of the staff-hunt. (Session 05)
- On entering, they came upon a party of **5 goblins, 1 human, and a cloaked figure**
  — which proved to be a **[draconian](../npcs/draconians.md) tracking party.** (Session 05)
- **⚔️ Site of the Session 05 draconian ambush.** The party fought and **killed the
  draconian tracking party here** — the goblins and human (Pip, Malix's Fireball, and
  Vaelor's summoned elk), the first cloaked figure, and **three more winged
  [draconians](../npcs/draconians.md)** that appeared to parley and then fought. **Four
  draconians dead** in the wood; the party kept the real [Staff](../items/the-staff.md),
  traded off [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md), and left combat. The fight went
  **loud** (a Fireball, an alerted search party) — the wood's undead or reinforcements may
  yet answer. (Session 05)

## Open questions

- What undead walk in Darken Wood, and do they answer to anyone? Does the noise of the
  ambush draw them?
- Was the draconian tracking party native to the wood, or did they follow the party's trail
  **into** it? (A looted map scrap shows they were tracking the party's wagon.)
