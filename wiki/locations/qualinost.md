## What we know

- An **elven city** in the **south-center of the region**, marked on the map with a
  distinct **ringed / target symbol** (concentric circles).
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- It lies within / at the edge of the elven realm of **[Qualinesti](qualinesti.md)**,
  south and west of [Haven](haven.md) and [Solace](solace.md).
  ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)

## Open questions

- What does the ringed symbol on the map denote — a capital, a ward, or something else?
- Who rules Qualinost, and how do the elves relate to [Haven](haven.md), the
  [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md), and the staff-hunt?
