# Xak Tsaroth

**The site referred to for the whole campaign only as "X.T." — full name revealed in
Session 06.** The party is still travelling there — **as of Session 08 they are roughly
two days out.**

## What we know

- **📍 As of Session 08 the party is roughly TWO DAYS SHORT of Xak Tsaroth**, per
  [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md). They have **not** arrived. *(An earlier note in this file
  recorded an arrival in Session 08; that was a mis-identification of
  [the Tomb of the Two Dragons](the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md), a separate site on the road.)*
- **"X.T." is Xak Tsaroth.** Named outright by the [Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md)
  in [Darken Wood](darken-wood.md), who directed the party to travel there. (Session 06)
- Referred to in the Ringmaster's ledger only by initials — **"do not speak the full
  name."** ([Ringmaster's Ledger — Torn Map Scrap / Old Road](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-map-scrap.md))
- Described as an **"old [site] below the green"** — consistent with the Session 06 reveal
  that the approach is **through a swamp.** ([Ringmaster's Ledger — Torn Map Scrap / Old Road](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-map-scrap.md))
- Marked on both the torn map scrap and the regional map, **east of Haven and Solace.**
  ([Burned Regional Map](../documents/regional-map-burned.md),
  [Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md))
- **The buyer insists "the Staff must not reach X.T."** — said twice.
  ([Ringmaster's Ledger — Torn Map Scrap / Old Road](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-map-scrap.md))
  This now stands in **direct conflict** with the Forest Master's instruction to bring
  [Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md) and the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) there. (Session 06)
- **The [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md) are there** — the relic the
  Forest Master sent the party to find. (Session 06)
- **It is protected by a great evil.** Unnamed and undescribed. (Session 06)
- **The direct road is drowned.** The overland route no longer exists; the approach is
  **through a swamp.** (Session 06)
- **There are stone roads beneath the water** — a submerged causeway that will carry
  travellers through the swamp if they know where it lies. The Forest Master **gave the
  party maps.** (Session 06)
- **The [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) is also seeking Mishakal's relics** —
  revealed when the party showed the Forest Master the confiscated
  [Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md) token. The journey is a **race.** (Session 06)
- **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) remembers a swamp from his eastern journey** — the
  quest on which he found the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md). Strong indication he has
  **already been to Xak Tsaroth**, and that the Staff came from there. (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **What is the great evil that guards it?** Is it the dragon army's, or older and
  independent of them?
- **Why did the buyer insist the Staff must not reach here** — do they fear what the Staff
  does at Xak Tsaroth, or what Xak Tsaroth does to the Staff? Is the buyer an enemy, or
  someone who knows something the party doesn't?
- **Is this where [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) found the Staff** — "at the lip of the
  morning"? And is it where the [draconians](../npcs/draconians.md) he half-remembers were?
- If the draconians' claim that the Staff "was taken from us" is a **lie**
  ([Forest Master](../npcs/the-forest-master.md), Session 06), were they instead
  **guarding** it here when Riverwind took it?
- Why must the full name not be spoken?
- Are **Toede's masters** ("people with sharper teeth") connected to Xak Tsaroth?
