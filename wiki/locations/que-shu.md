## What we know

- The **plains homeland / people** of the **Que-Shu**, to whom
  **[Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md)** (the Chieftain's daughter) and
  **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md)** belong. ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md),
  [The Song of Riverwind](../documents/song-of-riverwind.md))
- On the map it is labeled **"Que-sha"** and sits **just east / south-east of
  [Solace](solace.md).** ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- **[The Song of Riverwind](../documents/song-of-riverwind.md)** places the Que-Shu in
  **endless grasslands**: Goldmoon is "the princess," her father the chief, and Riverwind
  "a poor man's son" who was sent **east** on a quest for "strong magic" and returned with
  the blue [Staff](../items/the-staff.md); when the tribe moved to stone him, the Staff
  flared and the two vanished. (Session 04)

## Open questions

- Who is the **Que-Shu Chieftain** (Goldmoon's father), and where exactly do the
  Que-Shu dwell relative to the burning of Solace?
- Is Riverwind also Que-Shu, or from another plains people of the same grasslands?
