## What we know
- A settlement where travelers may be questioned; the Ringmaster warns to keep cargo hidden there. ([Ringmaster's Ledger — Special Cargo / Things Not To Say](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-special-cargo.md))
- "Haven listens for coin, prayer, and fear. Keep wagons quiet there." ([Ringmaster's Ledger — Roads / Payments / Witnesses](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-roads-payments.md))
- Appears on the regional map and the torn map scrap, linked by road to Solace and X.T. ([Burned Regional Map](../documents/regional-map-burned.md), [Ringmaster's Ledger — Torn Map Scrap / Old Road](../documents/ringmasters-ledger-map-scrap.md))
- **On the DM regional map, Haven sits center-left (to the south-west of [Solace](solace.md)).** [Darken Wood](darken-wood.md) lies **between Haven and Solace**, and a road runs **south from Solace toward Haven, passing Darken Wood.** The elven realm [Qualinesti](qualinesti.md) and its city [Qualinost](qualinost.md) lie further **west / south** of Haven. ([Regional Map: Solace–Haven](../documents/regional-map-solace-haven.md), Session 05)
- **Haven directs the staff-hunt.** Per [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md), the Seekers hunt the Staff on **orders from Haven**, and the goblin "allies" were **sent from Haven**; Haven and the Seekers' doctrine are commanded by **Lord Seeker Elistan.** (Session 05)
