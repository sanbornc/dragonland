# The Forest Master

**A unicorn** — the living power that rules [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md),
served by its centaurs. A being of the old, good, pre-Cataclysm order the world insists is
gone. **She** received the party in a clearing in Session 06 and set them on the road to
[Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md).

## What we know

- **She is a unicorn**, and the wood's ruling authority. Her **centaurs** surrounded the
  party's wagons after [Emrich](emrich.md)'s loop broke and escorted them to her.
  (Session 06)
- **The centaurs were watching.** Their leader's greeting — *"that did not turn out as I
  expected"* — means the wood knew about the loop and **expected the party to fail** or be
  swallowed by it. He seemed **impressed.** (Session 06)
- **The [friendly bears](the-friendly-bears.md) had already vouched for the party.** The two
  goblin-wounded bears the party spared in Session 03 **told the unicorn of their mercy** —
  the party was known to the wood before they ever entered it. (Session 06)
- **⭐ On the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md):** it is **not merely a weapon or a vessel of
  healing** — it is **"a promise that the gods have not forgotten the people; the people have
  forgotten the gods."** (Session 06)
- **✅ The [draconians](draconians.md)' claim that the Staff "was taken from us" is false**,
  and **[Goldmoon](goldmoon.md) is its rightful owner.** (Session 06)
- **⭐ The promise was [Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md)'s**, and **the vanished constellations
  represent the old gods** (Session 05: the Queen of Darkness and the Dragon). (Session 06)
- **🕯️ Her knowledge of the Disks came from a voice that spoke directly from her.** She
  implied she was *told* — she is a **conduit**, and something used her to speak.
  (Session 06)
- **She sent the party to [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md) to find the
  [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md)**, naming "X.T." outright and
  ending the campaign's oldest mystery. (Session 06)
- **Shown the confiscated Mishakal token**, she revealed that the **dragon army is seeking
  those relics as well** — making the journey a race. (Session 06)
- **She gave the party maps**, warning that **Xak Tsaroth is protected by a great evil**,
  that **the direct road is drowned**, and that **stone roads beneath the swamp water** will
  carry them through. (Session 06)
- **She allowed the party to leave their [wagons](../items/party-wagon.md) in her wood** —
  safe storage the [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md) cannot search and the dragon army
  cannot follow them into. (Session 06)
- **🎁 She granted a boon lasting until the battle with the evil at Xak Tsaroth:**
  **+1 to weapons** (taken by [Renob](../characters/renob.md) — longsword — and
  [Pip](../characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) — bow), an **extra 4th-level spell slot** each
  for [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) and
  [Malix](../characters/malix.md), and **extra radiant damage on Divine Smite** for
  [Vaelor](../characters/vaelor-grinward.md). Note the blessing **expires at** the
  confrontation it is meant for. (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **Whose voice spoke through her?** Presumed [Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md) — it is her
  Staff, her Disks, her promise — but the Forest Master did not name it. *(Ask outright.)*
- **Is the voice the same one that answers [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s
  prayers** — "a god whose name he is only beginning to recognize"?
- **Is "[the Queen](the-queen.md)" a god?** If the vanished *Queen of Darkness* is an old god
  entering the world, the war the party has tracked since Session 01 may be a **god's** war,
  not a kingdom's. *(Inference — not stated by her.)*
- **What is the great evil at Xak Tsaroth**, and how far ahead is the dragon army?
- How long has she ruled Darken Wood, and did she know [Emrich](emrich.md)'s knights were
  looping there all along?
- Was the loop a **test** the party unknowingly passed?
