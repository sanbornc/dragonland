# The Warlock at the Tomb

The **leader** of the party camped at
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)** in
[the Drowned Swamp](../locations/the-drowned-swamp.md). **Name unknown.**

> **Status: ALIVE and fighting.** Session 08 ended mid-combat with the warlock still up.

## What we know

- **He leads a camp** of **goblins** and at least **two mages**, pitched at the tomb with a
  **tent outside** it. (Session 08)
- **They are translating old stories.** Overheard from outside the tent: an **argument about
  old stories being translated**, and **a warning against using force.** (Session 08)
- **⚠️ They tried to ARREST [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)** when he
  walked up to talk — **not** kill him, and **not** seize the [Staff](../items/the-staff.md).
  They behave as though they hold **authority** at this site. Boilerdang ran. (Session 08)
- **He killed [Vaelor](../characters/vaelor-grinward.md)** inside the tomb;
  [Renob](../characters/renob.md) brought Vaelor back with a potion. (Session 08)
- **His two mages are dead** — killed by [Pip](../characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) and Vaelor
  inside the tomb. **Seven of his goblins** were killed by Boilerdang outside. (Session 08)

## Open questions

- **Who is he, and who does he answer to?** Is he with the
  [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) that sacked this tomb, a rival expedition, or
  a third party entirely?
- **"Arrest" on whose authority?** Nobody in this campaign has tried to arrest the party since
  [Solace](../locations/solace.md) — is he a [Seeker](../factions/the-seekers.md), or something
  else?
- **What is he translating, and why here?**
- **Who warned against using force, and about what?**
- **Can he be talked to?** The first attempt ended in an arrest attempt — but he is the only
  person here who knows what this place is.
