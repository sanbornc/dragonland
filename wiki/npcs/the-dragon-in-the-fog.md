# The Dragon in the Fog

**The campaign's first dragon.** Seen at a distance across
[the Drowned Swamp](../locations/the-drowned-swamp.md) in Session 07, moments after the party
killed [the Heart of the Swamp](the-heart-of-the-swamp.md).

## What we know

- **What they saw** (DM description, Session 07): at the far edge of the swamp, beyond the dead
  trees and drifting reeds, the fog shifted and something moved inside it — **not close**, far
  enough that only **scale** registered. A vast shadow passed behind the mist, and for a few
  seconds the outline became clear:
  > *A long neck. A horned head. Two enormous wings spreading through the fog. A dragon-shaped
  > silhouette standing somewhere out in the swamp.*
- **⚠️ It turned and looked directly toward the island**, then the fog rolled back across it
  and it was gone.
- **What they never saw or heard:** *"You never see its feet. You never hear a roar. You never
  even see it move clearly."* The DM's own framing was that the party know **what they think
  they just saw.**
- **It was standing in the swamp, not flying** — as far as could be told.
- **Mechanically** it forced a **DC 14 Wisdom saving throw**: on a failure, **frightened** until
  the end of the character's next turn, **unable to willingly move toward where it appeared.**
  Two characters rolled **natural 1s** and will not advance that way. (Session 07)
- **Context:** minutes earlier a [draconian](draconians.md) scout had watched the party's fight
  from the fog and **withdrawn without engaging.** The
  [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) is already in the swamp, racing the party for the
  [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md). (Session 07)
- **Nothing attacked during the party's long rest** afterward, despite both the scout and this
  creature knowing roughly where they were. (Session 07)

## Open questions

- **What colour, and whose?** Nothing about allegiance was established — only the shape.
- **Was it physically there at all?** No feet, no sound, no clear movement, and the wording
  *"what they think they just saw."* A body, a projection, or a warning.
- **Did it see them** — and does it know about [Goldmoon](goldmoon.md) and the
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md)? Had the draconian scout already reported?
- **Is it between the party and [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md)?** Two party members
  cannot willingly move toward it.
- **Is this the "great evil" the [Forest Master](the-forest-master.md) warned guards Xak
  Tsaroth** — or something separate?
- **Why did it let the party rest?**
