# The Heart of the Swamp

> **Status: DEAD** — killed by the party on the seventh island of
> [the Drowned Swamp](../locations/the-drowned-swamp.md), Session 07.

The ruling creature of [the Drowned Swamp](../locations/the-drowned-swamp.md), and the thing
the party had been standing on since the end of Session 06. Its name is a **title**, not a
species — the same shape of name as **[the Forest Master](the-forest-master.md)** of
[Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md). Each wild place in this region appears to have a keeper.

## What we know

- **It was a plant.** [Hederick](high-theocrat-hederick.md) described the corpse as *"a ruined
  mass of roots and tendrils"* and spat the word — *"A plant."* This is why the party's first
  sight of it, looking down through the water, read as **roots that moved.** (Session 07)
- **The seventh "island" was its body.** The party walked onto it, looted a corpse on it, and
  only realised when the ground **contracted** and a tendril rose. (Sessions 06–07)
- **Tentacles were separate HP pools**, roughly **85 each** — a single 43-damage hit left one
  *bloodied*. Killing tentacles did **not** damage the body. (Session 07)
- **Resistant to poison and acid** — revealed by [Pip](../characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md)'s
  Hunter's Mark. (Session 07)
- **Its arsenal:** (Session 07)
  - **Noxious gas clouds** — the **poisoned** condition in a **20-ft radius**, used repeatedly.
  - **Mud elementals**, summoned **at will** — it replaced them as fast as they were killed, so
    attrition was never a winning strategy.
  - **Water surges** across the island — modest damage (8) but they knocked the **entire party
    prone**, setting up the elementals.
  - Tentacle strikes from beneath the water, with the body submerged and out of line of sight.
- **⚔️ Killed by radiant damage.** [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s
  **Spirit Guardians** accounted for two tentacles, a mud elemental, and finally the Heart
  itself — he walked past the body and the aura finished it. Radiant bypassed everything it
  resisted; [Vaelor](../characters/vaelor-grinward.md)'s
  [Forest Master](the-forest-master.md) boon (extra radiant on Divine Smite) landed for the
  same reason. (Session 07)
- **🗺️ Its death thinned the fog.** The swamp cleared noticeably once it was dead — the mist
  appears to have been part of it, or its doing. (Session 07)
- **It never killed anybody.** Across seven-plus rounds against a party that was **prone,
  poisoned and out of position**, it dealt only modest damage and spent its turns on gas and
  summons. It behaved like something **pushing intruders off its island**, not feeding.
  *(Inference — the DM did not state its intent.)*
- **It very likely killed the dead [Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md)**
  found on the same island in Session 06 — a body left where it fell, on the one road through.
  *(Inference.)*

## Open questions

- **Was it evil, or a guardian?** The party killed the swamp's counterpart to the
  [Forest Master](the-forest-master.md) — who is the one who **sent them down this road.**
  Does she learn of it, and does it matter?
- **Could it have been spoken to?** The party never tried. Darken Wood was crossed by mercy,
  not by force.
- **Was it what kept the [dragon army](../factions/black-banners.md) out of the swamp?** It sat
  on the only causeway to [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md), killing what used it.
  **The party removed it.**
- **What was the fog-producing refuse on the fourth island** — its doing, its feeding, or
  something else living out here?
- Is there anything to recover from the corpse? *(Loot pending from the DM.)*
