# Emrich

A **[Knight of Solamnia](../factions/knights-of-solamnia.md)** who died at the
**Cataclysm** and spent three centuries repeating the battle he fell in, deep in
[Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md) — until
[Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) absolved him. **At rest.**

## What we know

- **A Solamnic knight leading roughly 50 knights** holding a battle line against endless
  waves of **shadow figures** in Darken Wood. (Session 06)
- **He mistook the party for new recruits** and ordered them to **"hold the line."** On the
  second cycle he gave his name: **Emrich.** (Session 06)
- **The battle was a loop.** He **faded away mid-conversation** and the whole engagement
  **reset** — same knight, same conscription offer, **no memory of the previous cycle.**
  (Session 06)
- **He believed the year was the year of the Cataclysm**, and that he was under orders to
  hold against "the enemy." He was fighting inside the moment the world broke. (Session 06)
- **Told the truth, he was not surprised** — on some level he already knew. (Session 06)
- **His confession: he sounded the horn for retreat when the mountain fell.** At the moment
  of the Cataclysm, Emrich — who held the horn — **called the retreat rather than the
  stand.** (Session 06)
- **He believed the loop was his punishment** for that order: condemned to hold a line
  forever, never able to retreat and never able to win. (Session 06)
- **🕊️ Boilerdang absolved him**, convincing him that **sounding the retreat was not a
  failure of the oath** — with the world ending, the order that got men out alive was the
  right one. Emrich accepted it. **The knights faded into the forest and the shadow figures
  disappeared.** (Session 06)
- The loop's cause was **guilt, not the wood.** A three-century haunting that ~50 armed
  knights could not end by fighting was ended by **a priest's absolution.** (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **Is there a horn** — still on him, still somewhere in Darken Wood? It is the object the
  entire haunting turned on.
- Was the "punishment" self-imposed, or laid on him by something or someone?
- What **enemy** was he actually ordered to hold against, in the year of the Cataclysm?
  (Never answered — and the shadow figures may have been it.)
- Are the knights truly at rest, or elsewhere in the wood? Did anything remain on the
  ground — armour, blades, remains that could be buried?
- **What did the [centaurs](the-forest-master.md) know?** Their leader said *"that did not
  turn out as I expected"* — they were watching, and expected the party to fail.
