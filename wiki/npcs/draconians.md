# Draconians

Winged, reptilian, cloaked humanoids first encountered in
[Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md) (Session 05). Almost certainly the
[Dragon Prophecy](../lore/the-dragon-prophecy.md)'s **"scaled children of forgotten
fire,"** now walking the world as the **Dragon constellation vanishes from the sky.**
[Malix](../characters/malix.md) (who speaks Draconic) and a recovered **scale** named
them: **draconians.**

## What we know

- **Appearance:** winged, reptilian, cloaked humanoids. They travel hooded/cloaked
  and can pass at a distance as a "cloaked figure." (Session 05)
- **Fire-resistant.** [Malix](../characters/malix.md)'s Fireball, which killed the
  goblins and badly wounded the human, **barely marked** the first draconian — the
  first hint at its nature. (Session 05)
- **They die into stone — trapping the weapon that killed them.** When
  [Vaelor](../characters/vaelor-grinward.md) struck the first with his axe it **turned
  to stone**, trapping the axe in the now-stone body; the last one **petrified on the
  elk's horn.** This is the hallmark **Baaz-type** draconian death (a slain Baaz turns
  to stone, potentially trapping the blade). Killing them with **spell damage** (the
  Spirit Guardians aura) sidesteps the weapon-trapping. (Session 05)
- **Radiant / divine damage is the counter — where fire fails.**
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)'s **Spirit Guardians**
  (radiant) **bloodied two of three** draconians in a single cast; the third only saved
  for half. The dragon-kin shrug off fire but **not** the very holy power the Seekers
  brand heresy — the light answers the dark. (Session 05)
- **They serve a black-claw military — the [Black Banners](../factions/black-banners.md).**
  Recovered from the hunting party: **shields marked with a black claw symbol**, a strip
  of **black leather with claw-like military markings**, **command whistles**, and
  **Draconic order-tokens** — a coordinated army unit, not wild monsters. Matches the
  prophecy's *"iron will march beneath black banners."* (Session 05)
- **They were tracking the party's wagon trail.** A looted **map scrap** marked the
  party's own wagon route in charcoal — this was a **targeted** tracking party, not a
  chance patrol. (Session 05)
- **Their standing orders:** a Draconic order-token (read by [Malix](../characters/malix.md))
  reads **"Staff intact. Woman alive. Others expendable."** — recover the
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) undamaged, take [Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md) **alive**,
  kill everyone else. (Session 05)
- **They claim the Staff "was taken from us."** When the party protested the
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) wasn't theirs to give, the draconians answered *"that is
  where you are wrong — it was **taken from us**."* — asserting prior ownership. (A claim,
  not confirmed truth.) (Session 05)
- **They know [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md) by name** and offered
  to **parley** ("Boilerdang, let's talk") even after the party had killed one of their
  kind. Their price: hand over [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) and the Staff.
  (Session 05)
- **They can tell the real Staff from a fake.** A strong Deception and a crude forgery
  fooled them only for a moment; on inspection they saw through it and combat resumed —
  consistent with their claim of prior ownership. (Session 05)
- **They enforced Hederick's death-mark.** On receiving him at the handoff they
  **immediately stabbed** [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) — they wanted the
  marked theocrat dead. (Boilerdang later revived him.) (Session 05)
- **[Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) recognized them — from a distant memory.** As he
  drew closer he knew them from somewhere in his past, though faintly. This dovetails with
  their "taken from us" claim and Riverwind's **eastern quest** for the Staff
  ([Song of Riverwind](../documents/song-of-riverwind.md)) — his half-forgotten journey may
  be where he first met **both** the draconians and the Staff. (Session 05)
- **Body count:** the party **killed 4** in Darken Wood — the first cloaked figure plus
  the three that appeared to parley. (Session 05)

- **❌ Their claim on the Staff is a LIE.** The **[Forest Master](the-forest-master.md)** of
  [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md) — a unicorn — states flatly that the draconians'
  *"it was taken from us"* is **false**, and that **[Goldmoon](goldmoon.md) is the Staff's
  rightful owner.** The [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) is a promise left by
  **[Mishakal](../lore/mishakal.md)**. They lied to the party's faces about provenance —
  which suggests they know **exactly** what it is. (Session 06)
- **They are a "dragon army," and they are collecting Mishakal's relics.** Shown the
  confiscated Mishakal token, the Forest Master revealed the **dragon army is also seeking
  the [Disks of Mishakal](../quests/the-disks-of-mishakal.md)** at
  [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md). They are not merely hunting one Staff — they
  are gathering the goddess's relics wholesale, which would strip away the evidence that the
  gods ever answered. (Session 06)

## Open questions

- Are these truly the prophecy's **"scaled children of forgotten fire"** — and is this
  **first contact** with the dragons' foretold return?
- **Where has [Riverwind](../npcs/riverwind.md) seen them before?** His eastern quest may
  hold the shared origin of both the draconians and the Staff. **If they never owned the
  Staff, were they *guarding* it at [Xak Tsaroth](../locations/xak-tsaroth.md) when he took
  it?** *(Inference, Session 06.)*
- **Why lie about owning it** — to make the party feel like thieves, or to conceal that they
  are collecting a goddess?
- **Are they the "great evil" at Xak Tsaroth, or does something older wait there?** (Session 06)
- **How far ahead of the party are they in the race for the Disks?** (Session 06)
- How many more units carry the same standing orders? This was **one** tracking party of
  a black-claw army.
- Are they Haven's dreaded **"allies"** — the ones who leave priests afraid to speak?
