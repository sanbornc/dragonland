# The Archaeologist

The one person from the camp at
**[the Tomb of the Two Dragons](../locations/the-tomb-of-the-two-dragons.md)** who **did not
run.** **Name unknown.**

> **Status: ALIVE, approaching, and NOT INTERESTED IN FIGHTING.** Walked calmly toward the
> tomb after the dragon exploded, while everyone else in the camp fled.

## What we know

- **🚶 They did not run.** When the "dragon" **overpressured and exploded**, **the rest of the
  camp fled** — this one **started walking calmly toward the building.** (Session 09)
- **🔍 They are an ARCHAEOLOGIST.** (Session 09)
- **🕊️ They are not interested in fighting.** (Session 09)
- **They walked toward four armed PCs** who had just destroyed the machine and killed the
  camp's mages — **unhurried.** (Session 09)

## Probable connection to Session 08 *(inference — not stated at the table)*

Before the fighting started, [Pip](../characters/pip-tumblewhistle.md) overheard people in the
tent outside the tomb **arguing about old stories that are being translated**, and **a warning
against using force.** A non-combatant archaeologist fits that overheard scene — and would
explain why the camp tried to **arrest** [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md)
rather than kill him. **Whether this person was one of those voices, or the one who warned
against force, is not established.**
