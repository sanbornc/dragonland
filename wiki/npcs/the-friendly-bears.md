# The Friendly Bears

## What we know

- **Two bears** the party met in a forest on the way to [Solace](../locations/solace.md).
  They had been **wounded by goblins** and, agitated, were **chasing two
  travelers**. (Session 03)
- The party **de-escalated the encounter through Animal Handling and healing the
  bears**. The bears calmed and are now **friendly / well-disposed toward the
  party.** (Session 03)
- The bears then **retreated into the woods before the party reached Solace** — so
  they were **not present** for the roadblock fight. (Session 03)

- **🐻 They vouched for the party.** In [Darken Wood](../locations/darken-wood.md)
  (Session 06) the two bears reappeared in the [Forest Master](the-forest-master.md)'s
  clearing — they had **already told the unicorn of the party's mercy.** The kindness shown
  in Session 03 is what bought the party standing before the power that rules the wood, and
  explains why the centaurs **surrounded but did not attack.**
- **🧿 The large bear (female) was wearing an amulet — and gave it to
  [Boilerdang](../characters/boilerdang-criddlycrotch.md).** She **growled in his face**,
  then indicated he should take it. It is the
  **[Amulet of the Remembered Paw](../items/amulet-of-the-remembered-paw.md)**: **once per
  long rest it summons one of the bears to fight for the party for 10 minutes.** The bears'
  goodwill is now a **callable ally.** (Session 06)
- **A wild bear does not wear jewellery** — someone **placed** that amulet on her, and she
  carried it until she had someone to give it to. (Session 06)

## Open questions

- **Who hung the amulet on her, and why?** Was she carrying it *for* someone?
- Are the **goblin wounds** they bore in Session 03 connected to something hunting the
  amulet?
- What kind of bears, and where exactly in the forest?
- Are the goblins that wounded them the same **[Seeker](../factions/the-seekers.md)
  goblins** manning the Solace roadblock (Session 03), the **Black Banner–hired
  goblins** from the tower and excavation site (Session 02), or an unrelated band?
- Does the bears' goodwill translate into aid — fighting alongside the party,
  guiding them, or following?
