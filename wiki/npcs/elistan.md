# Lord Seeker Elistan

Named by [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) under interrogation
(Session 05) as the commander of **[Haven](../locations/haven.md)** and of the
[Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)' **doctrine** — Hederick's superior and the head
behind the whole staff-hunt. Not yet met by the party.

## What we know

- **He commands [Haven](../locations/haven.md) and the [Seekers](../factions/the-seekers.md)'
  doctrine.** [High Theocrat Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) is his
  **subordinate** — Hederick is one theocrat under Elistan, not the top of the faith.
  (Session 05, Hederick's interrogation)
- **He is the head behind the staff-hunt.** The hunt for [Goldmoon](../npcs/goldmoon.md)'s
  [Staff](../items/the-staff.md) is directed from Haven, under Elistan's command — not
  Hederick's local initiative. (Session 05)
- **His doctrine condemns those healed by true divine power — a death-mark.** By being
  cured with the Staff, [Hederick](../npcs/high-theocrat-hederick.md) was **marked for
  death** under Elistan's doctrine; being healed by "witch" / true divine power is itself
  a **capital heresy.** Hederick warns the **whole party is now marked**, and that if
  Elistan has marked you for death, **you will die.** This recasts Hederick's Session 04
  self-maiming and suicide: to a true believer, being healed by the Staff was **damnation**,
  not rescue. (Session 05) — see [The Silent Gods & the Seekers](../quests/the-silent-gods.md)

## Open questions

- **How far does Elistan's reach extend** — and can a death-mark be **lifted or outrun**,
  or does it follow the party into every town under Haven's control?
- Is Elistan a true believer, a cynic wielding doctrine as a weapon, or something else?
- How does he relate to the **draconians / [Black Banners](../factions/black-banners.md)**
  — master, servant, or unwitting ally? The draconians want the same Staff Elistan hunts.
- Does he command the **"Highseekers of Solace"** who issued the
  [condemnation notice](../documents/highseekers-condemnation-notice.md)?
